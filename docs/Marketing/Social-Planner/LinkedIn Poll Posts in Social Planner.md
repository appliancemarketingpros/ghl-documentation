You can now create and publish LinkedIn Polls directly from Social Planner.

### **What's Supported**

  * Create native LinkedIn polls from the composer

  * Add a question (up to 140 characters)

  * Add 2–4 answer options (up to 30 characters each)

  * Choose a poll duration of 1, 3, 7, or 14 days

  * Preview your poll before publishing


###   


### **Supported Workflows**

LinkedIn polls can be used with:

  * Instant publishing

  * Scheduled posts

  * Recurring posts

  * Category Queue

  * CSV Bulk Upload

  * Drafts


###   


### **Important Notes**

  * Polls cannot be combined with images, videos, PDFs, or other media in the LinkedIn post.

  * When creating content for multiple platforms, media can still be added to non-LinkedIn platform variants.

  * If media is attached to a LinkedIn post, the poll will not be published.


### How to Create a LinkedIn Poll

  1. Go to **Marketing → Social Planner**

  2. Create a new post and select a LinkedIn account

  3. Click **Add Poll**

  4. Enter your question and answer options and Select a poll duration
