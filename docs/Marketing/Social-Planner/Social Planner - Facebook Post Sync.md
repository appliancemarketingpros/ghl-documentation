# **What is Facebook Post Sync in Social Planner?**

  


Facebook Post Sync allows HighLevel to import your existing Facebook Page posts into Social Planner so you can view, manage, and analyze past content alongside newly scheduled and published posts. This helps make Social Planner a more complete source of truth for social publishing and analytics. To use this feature, your Facebook account must be connected.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Facebook Post Sync**

  


  * **Centralized post management:** View past Facebook posts inside Social Planner instead of managing historical content separately in Facebook.  
  

  * **No manual recreation:** Bring recent existing posts into Social Planner without recreating them one by one.  
  

  * **Better performance visibility:** Review synced post insights in Statistics to understand how past content performed.  
  

  * **Improved reporting accuracy:** Keep more Facebook publishing history inside one reporting workflow.  
  

  * **Faster onboarding:** Connect accounts and quickly bring in recent post history when setting up a new location or client.


* * *

## **Facebook Post Sync Settings and Behavior**

  


A few setup and platform behaviors affect what users see after enabling sync, especially during the first import and during future refresh cycles.

  


  * Facebook Post Sync only works for **connected** **Facebook** **Pages**.  
  


  * The standard sync window includes the **last** **30** **days** of posts.  
  


  * The first sync may **retrieve** **additional** **historical** **posts** where available.  
  


  * Sync **continues** **automatically** in the **background** after setup.  
  


  * Insights **refresh** **every** **24** **hours** rather than continuously.  
  


  * Reconnecting Facebook access may be required if permissions expire.


* * *

## **What Gets Synced**

  


Facebook Post Sync imports recent Facebook Page content and processes supported media automatically so your existing posts can be viewed inside Social Planner.

  


  * Posts from the **last 30 days** are synced into Social Planner.  
  


  * Posts are **synced up to the current day** based on **UTC** **time**.  
  


  * The initial sync may fetch additional historical posts where available.  
  


  * **Text** , **images** , and **videos** are supported.  
  


  * Media is processed automatically during import.  
  


  * Media processing uses **batch** **handling** to support smoother imports.  
  


  * **Video** **sync** is **supported** up to platform limits.

## **How To Enable Facebook Post Sync**

  


A proper setup ensures your Facebook account is connected, sync is enabled correctly, and historical posts can begin importing without interruption.

  


### **_Method 1:_**_Enable Post Sync While Connecting a Social Account_

  


You can enable Post Sync directly while connecting your Facebook account.

  


  1. Go to **Marketing** >**Social Planner.**  
  


  2. Click **\+ Socials.**  
  
**![](https://jumpshare.com/share/aS1QNSBzd7cJbEF07JA2+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+18.43.03.png)**  
  


  3. In the**Connect Socials popup** , enable the toggle: **Sync Posts Automatically.**  
**  
**

  4. Click**Connect** Facebook and finish linking your account.  
  


  5. Once enabled, Social Planner will begin syncing your Facebook posts automatically.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/o8MPHuGY7b6deIXrNwGg+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+18.45.54.png)  
  


### **_Method 2:_**_Enable Post Sync from Social Planner Settings_

  


If your Facebook account is already connected, you can enable Post Sync later.

  


  1. Navigate to **Marketing > Social Planner.**  
  


  2. Click on **Settings.**  
  
**![](https://jumpshare.com/share/Ce1hHjIktHaej7yOtI4t+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+19.18.59.png)**  
  


  3. Go to**Social Accounts > Facebook**.  
  


  4. Enable the **Sync Posts** toggle for the page you want**.**  
  


  5. The system will start syncing posts in the background.

  
**![](https://jumpshare.com/share/83seeVdXPYGc55x8fyXd+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+19.21.48.png)**  
  


  6. Once sync is enabled, open**Planner** to review synced posts.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/GQQiZkl6Xaf3hfTrGWHp+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+18.47.47.png)  
  

  7. Open **Statistics** to review synced performance data.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/QYHEnVX545BjQTd6rHmD+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+18.50.09.png)  
  

  8. If you see a **disconnect** or **expired** -**access****message** , follow the reconnect prompt to resume syncing.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/IiUOWrWI9oXjXSiip4YM+/Screen+Shot+2026-04-21+at+18.51.09.png)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: How far back will Facebook posts sync?**  
Social Planner automatically syncs **Facebook posts from the last 30 days**. In some cases, an extended historical fetch may occur during the initial sync if supported by the platform.

  


**Q: How often do synced posts update?**  
Post insights and analytics **refresh every 24 hours** to stay within Facebook API limits while keeping your data up to date.

  


**Q: What happens if my Facebook account disconnects?**  
If your Facebook connection expires, syncing will pause. You’ll see a **reconnect prompt** in Social Planner simply reconnect your account to resume syncing.

  


**Q: Can I disable Post Sync later?**  
Yes. You can **turn off the “Sync Posts Automatically” toggle** anytime from Social Planner settings. This will stop future syncing but will not delete already imported posts.

  


**Q: What types of Facebook content are supported?**  
Synced content supports text, images, and videos.
