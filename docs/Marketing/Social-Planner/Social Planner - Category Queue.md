HighLevel's Category Queue feature helps you automate and repurpose evergreen social content. Group posts under a category, define a recurring publishing schedule, and let the queue cycle through the posts continuously. This article explains how Category Queues work, how to create and manage them, and how to control future content.

## **What Is a Category Queue?**

  


A Category Queue lets you group social posts under a category and publish them according to a recurring schedule. After the queue reaches the final post, it starts again from the beginning, making it useful for evergreen content.

You can build a queue from published posts assigned to a category, add new posts while configuring the queue, and automatically include future posts created under the same category.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Category Queues**

  


  * **Automated evergreen scheduling:** Publish category-based content continuously without manually rescheduling every post.  
  

  * **Reusable content:** Repurpose published posts and include future posts assigned to the same category.  
  

  * **Priority control:** Choose whether new content appears at the beginning or end of the queue.  
  

  * **Custom schedules:** Set specific publishing days and time slots for each queue.  
  

  * **Flexible queue management:** Edit, reorder, pause, resume, reschedule, or delete a queue as needed.

## **Before You Begin**

  

    
    
    **Note:** To create a queue from existing content, make sure the published posts are assigned to the appropriate social category. You can also create a new category and add posts while setting up the queue.

## **How to Create a Category Queue**

  


  1. Log in to your sub-account.  
  

  2. Go to **Marketing** > **Social Planner**.  
  
![Navigate to Social Planner from the Marketing menu](https://jumpshare.com/share/Pc9OwGeP0CQNwDqQGxDI+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-24+at+10.50.25+PM.png)  
  

  3. Click **\+ New Post** , then select **Category Queue**.  
  
![Select Category Queue from the New Post menu](https://jumpshare.com/share/8c1m6djAWSQvyC42Ytl2+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-24+at+11.07.43+PM.png)  
  

  4. Under **Select a Category** , choose the category you want to use for the queue.  
  
![Select an existing social category for the queue](https://jumpshare.com/share/qbxG3xek8G9cvNa14qm6+/CN96DupK6jOf3xVoh6AdGV2sZqbOoSTBkQ.jpeg)  
  

  5. To create a category, enter a new category name and select the option to add it.  
  
![Create a new social category while setting up a queue](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060860653/original/VICQYWl3jqjF_J-r8Rp0z-lF_L85LMWg5Q.png?1765991958)  
  

  6. Enable **Enable future posts queuing** to automatically add future posts assigned to the category to this queue.  
  

  7. Enable **Prioritize new content** to add newly queued content to the beginning of the queue. When disabled, new content is added to the end.  
  

  8. Click **Continue**.  
  
![Continue after selecting the category and queue preferences](https://jumpshare.com/share/NcCpZOrNCiOcoM56an2w+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-24+at+11.46.49+PM.png)  
  

  9. Select the publishing days and time slots for the queue.  
  

  10. Click **Queue Posts** to apply the schedule and continue to the queue editor.  
  
![Set the publishing schedule and continue to the queue editor](https://jumpshare.com/share/6p1qoeG3RInnMgludW8d+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-24+at+11.49.18+PM.png)  
  

  11. Review the queued posts. Edit the post content or media as needed.  
  

  12. To add another post, click **New Post**. You can then drag the post to the preferred position in the queue.  
  

  13. Add multiple content variations when you want the queue to rotate different versions of a post.  
  
![Add content variations to a post in a Category Queue](https://jumpshare.com/share/Z8q8ZI64iYqMe5h0QpxB+/GIF+Recording+2025-11-25+at+7.17.11+PM.gif)  
  

  14. Review all caution, warning, and error messages, and resolve any identified issues.  
  

  15. When no blocking errors remain, click **Queue Posts** to activate the queue.  
  
![Resolve queue errors and activate the Category Queue](https://jumpshare.com/share/J5Lsc81T3q8Al1fhDM9n+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-25+at+7.03.11+PM.png)  
  

  16. Before activating the queue, you can select **Save as Draft** , **Delete** , or **Reset** , as needed.  
  
![Save, delete, or reset a Category Queue](https://jumpshare.com/share/Llasxm2JW3kAaDJTeQTp+/Screen+Shot+2025-11-25+at+7.05.00+PM.png)  
  

  17. To pause or resume the Category Queue later, go to the **Content** tab and use the queue status toggle.  
  
![Pause or resume a Category Queue from the Content tab](https://jumpshare.com/share/5RTw8IW6FJ7agWsJNyDs+/2hAzCIIduz8i2sS6g_8TQKy0WWJV6xY6eA.jpeg)

## **How to Manage a Category Queue**

  


Go to **Marketing** > **Social Planner** > **Content** > **Category Queue**. Locate the queue, then click the **More options** icon to view the available management actions.

  


![Category Queue More options menu in Social Planner](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861302/original/0_Bh0pGd3Mn6ZujZ9FiubHRzUCGT6W0R_g.png?1765992875)  
  


### **1\. Edit a Category Queue**

  


Select **Edit queue** to update the queue without deleting or recreating it. You can edit:

  * Post details, captions, media, and variations
  * Publishing frequency
  * Queue time slots
  * Post order by dragging and dropping items  
  


### **2\. Configure Queue Preferences**

  


Select **Queue preferences** from the **More options** menu. The available settings control how future posts are added to the queue.  
  


**Setting**| **When enabled**| **When disabled**  
---|---|---  
**Enable future posts queuing**|  Automatically adds new posts created under the category to the queue.| Future posts are not automatically added to the queue.  
**Prioritize new content**|  Adds new content to the beginning of the queue so it publishes first.| Adds new content to the end of the queue.  
  
  
  


![Queue preferences option in the Category Queue menu](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861428/original/raCU9zXB_zDWhZXW26mheyXJqRNBqLpaaQ.png?1765993165)  
  


![Enable future posts queuing and Prioritize new content settings](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861440/original/ME71MM_VsLUBQ32yGp-MItMmFsrGYroTag.png?1765993199)  
  


### **3\. View the Queue Calendar**

  


Select **View queue calendar** to review the queue in weekly or monthly view. Use the calendar to see how posts are distributed and identify scheduling gaps or content-heavy days.

  


### **4\. Reschedule the Entire Queue**

  


Select **Reschedule queue** to move the entire queue to a new start date. HighLevel recalculates the post dates based on the queue's existing publishing frequency and time slots.  
  


![Reschedule queue option in Social Planner](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861506/original/B-rKvVdwo3Ba2sjFuTy_MomIyZxXhekw3A.png?1765993274)  
  


![Select a new start date for a Category Queue](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861546/original/26ZksYwNAv7d-SEUJtty5NKelnd9UQtbDA.png?1765993329)  
  


### **5\. Add Posts from the Post Composer**

  


While creating a social post, select **Add to Queue** and choose a category.  
  


  * Selecting an existing category adds the post to that category's queue.  
  

  * Creating a new category creates a corresponding category queue for the post.  
  


![Add a social post to an existing or new Category Queue](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155063016594/original/FS8wPfJv0ra9CbjnufIbaucaLvlj_uz7eA.png?1768888475)  
  


### **6\. Pause, Resume, or Delete a Queue**

  


  * Use the status toggle on the **Category Queue** page to pause or resume a queue.  
  

  * Select **Delete** from the **More options** menu to remove a queue that you no longer need.  
  

  * Use **Reset** while editing a queue to discard changes to a queue item and restore its original content.

## **Important Notes**

  


  * Category Queues can include previously published posts assigned to a category. You can also create new posts and add them to the queue.  
  

  * Rescheduling an individual post in a Category Queue is not supported.  
  

  * You cannot reschedule or rearrange posts directly from the Category Queue calendar.  
  

  * Changes made under **Queue preferences** take effect from the next queue cycle.  
  

  * You can pause, resume, or delete a Category Queue from the **Content** tab.  
  

  * To review category and queue status, go to **Marketing > Social Planner > Settings > Categories.** The status column shows whether the queue is active, paused, or in draft.

## **Category Queue Public API**

  


If you want to create or manage Category Queues programmatically for custom workflows, integrations, or automation, you can use the **Category Queue Public API**.

Find the API reference in **Public API Docs → Social Planner → Category Queue**.

Authenticate with your **Location API key** or an **OAuth token** , depending on your integration type.

  


### **Common Supported Actions**

  


Action| Description  
---|---  
Fetch categories| Fetch available categories and their queue status.  
Manage Category Queues| Create, list, update, and fetch Category Queues.  
Manage queue items| Add, update, reset, and delete queue items.  
View calendar data| View scheduled posts in calendar formats.  
Advance the queue| Delete the currently active post and schedule the next available post.  
  
  


For endpoint details, request and response schemas, and examples, use the official documentation.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: How do I automatically add future posts to a Category Queue?**

Open **Queue preferences** and enable **Enable future posts queuing**. HighLevel then automatically adds new posts created under that category to the queue.  
  


**Q: How do I publish newly added content before existing queue items?**

Enable **Prioritize new content** under **Queue preferences**. New content is then added to the beginning of the queue.  
  


**Q: Can I change a Category Queue's schedule?**

Yes. Use **Edit queue** to change the publishing frequency or time slots. Use **Reschedule queue** to move the entire queue to a new start date. Rescheduling an individual queue post is not supported.  
  


**Q: How do I add a new post to an existing queue?**

Open the queue and select **New Post** , or select **Add to Queue** from the social post composer and choose the category. You can then drag the post to the preferred position while editing the queue.  
  


**Q: Can I add multiple content variations?**

Yes. Add variations to rotate different versions of the post's content and media.  
  


**Q: Can I change the order of posts later?**

Yes. Select **Edit queue** , then drag and drop the posts into the preferred order.
