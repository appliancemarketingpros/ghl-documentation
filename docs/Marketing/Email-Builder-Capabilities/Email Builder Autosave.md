# Email Builder Autosave

Protect your email templates and campaigns with automatic version saving

What You'll Learn

The email builder autosave feature protects your work by automatically saving edits during your session. This prevents data loss from accidental clicks, network issues, page refreshes, or navigation gestures.

This article explains how autosave works, how to enable it, and how to manage version history for your email templates and campaigns.

## What is Email Builder Autosave?

Email Builder Autosave automatically saves your work as you edit email templates and campaigns in HighLevel. Instead of manually saving after each change, the system creates version snapshots during your editing session.

This feature protects against common causes of data loss including accidental clicks outside the editor, network interruptions, browser refreshes, and navigation gestures like Mac's left-swipe.

The autosave indicator displays the timestamp of your last saved version. You can hover over this indicator to see exactly when your work was preserved, and access Version History to restore previous states if needed.

2

## Key Benefits

Autosave provides continuous protection for your email marketing work:

**Automatic Version Control** — All edits are saved as versions during your session without requiring manual saves

**Data Loss Prevention** — Protects against accidental page refreshes, network issues, and unintended navigation

**Last Save Timestamp** — Hover over the autosave indicator to see exactly when your work was last preserved

**Version History Access** — Review and restore previous saved states of your templates and campaigns

**Flexible Control for Support** — Support team members can disable autosave for their sessions when needed

## How to Enable Autosave

Autosave is currently available through HighLevel's Beta Labs. Follow these steps to enable and test the feature:

Step 1

Access Beta Labs

Navigate to Beta Labs in your HighLevel account settings and locate the autosave feature for the email builder.

Step 2

Enable the Feature

Toggle autosave on for the email builder.

## Using Version History

Version History allows you to review and restore previous saved states of your email templates and campaigns. Each autosave creates a timestamped version that you can revisit.

To access Version History, open the email builder for your template or campaign and locate the Version History option. This displays all saved versions with timestamps, allowing you to compare changes and restore earlier states if needed.

Tip

Version History is especially useful when you need to recover from unwanted changes or compare different design approaches during your editing session.

5

## Disabling Autosave (Support Users)

Support team members have access to a toggle control that allows them to disable autosave for their individual sessions. This flexibility is useful when troubleshooting issues or demonstrating specific behaviors without automatic version creation.

The autosave toggle appears in the email builder interface for users with support permissions. Disabling autosave affects only the current session and does not impact autosave settings for other users or locations.

Note

Autosave will be enabled by default for all locations. Users can still disable it through Labs settings if needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to manually save my work after enabling autosave?

No. Once autosave is enabled, the system automatically creates version snapshots during your editing session. You can continue working without manually clicking save buttons.

Q: How often does autosave create versions?

Autosave creates versions automatically during your editing session. You can hover over the autosave indicator to see the timestamp of the last saved version.

Q: Can I restore a previous version of my email?

Yes. Access Version History from the email builder to see all saved versions with timestamps. You can review and restore any previous state of your template or campaign.

Q: Will autosave be enabled automatically after June 1?

Yes. After June 1, autosave will be enabled by default for all locations. If you need to disable it, you can do so through Labs settings.

Q: Does autosave protect against browser crashes or network interruptions?

Yes. Autosave is designed to protect your work from data loss caused by accidental page refreshes, network issues, browser crashes, and unintended navigation gestures.

Q: Can support users disable autosave for troubleshooting?

Yes. Support team members can use a toggle in the email builder to disable autosave for their individual sessions. This does not affect autosave settings for other users.

Q: Where can I find the autosave setting currently?

Autosave is currently available in Beta Labs. Navigate to Labs settings and enable the autosave feature for the email builder.

Q: Does autosave work for both email templates and campaigns?

Yes. Autosave protects your work on both email templates and email campaigns in the HighLevel email builder.
