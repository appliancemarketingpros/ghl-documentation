# Check-in Experience for In Person Events

**Source URL:** [https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008294-check-in-experience-for-in-person-events](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008294-check-in-experience-for-in-person-events)  
**Category:** Marketing  
**Folder:** Events

---

Check-in allows event organizers to mark attendees as arrived at their venue using the mobile app. It works for **in-person****** events only—both ticketed and RSVP'd. For online events, you can view the attendee list, but check-in is not available.

There are two ways to check in an attendee: scanning their QR code or doing it manually.

  1. ## QR Code Check-in


Every attendee who purchases a ticket or registers via RSVP receives a ticket with a QR code. To check them in, open the mobile app and scan the QR code when they arrive.

  * Go to the Apps Section in Mobile > Select the Events App.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075254696/original/NH0e4fjzzRVW6s13FPBXt25617VfpVbThQ.png?1783322547)  
  

  * Select the Event you want to check in the attendees for  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075254787/original/qRkJlIw0gpSFmnDlPM3MzpcpdOsy6tQzoA.png?1783322588)  
  

  * Click on the "**Scan to check in** " to check in the attendees.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075254821/original/df4CMcEtraajPmwO2HtSi1lUjdHQtJugUg.png?1783322609)  
  

  * QR Code is scanned and attendee is checked in.  
  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075254832/original/STzqJLhamhkr3rMMrN6pDjKGVbJFBLqUYA.png?1783322631)  


  2. ## Manual Check-in


If QR scanning isn't working, or the attendee has lost their ticket, you can check them in manually. Verify their identity, then:

  * Go to the Attendees tab and tap on the Attendee.  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075252560/original/ZCiDAFA-5yw-HWz0UK7zcnPX1gKdfQN9iA.png?1783320530)  
  

  * Check in the attendee by clicking on the "**Check in** " button.  
  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075254851/original/Ttx21KeXcME1uVfOa6Q9Jac_aOBP-5yKPQ.png?1783322643)  

  * From the same view, you can also see the attendee's form submission details.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155075252832/original/mOHp9r_La-90izXL5-uKDisgL8rsU3EK3w.png?1783320907)
