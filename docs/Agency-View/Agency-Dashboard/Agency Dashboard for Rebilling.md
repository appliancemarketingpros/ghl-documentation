## **What is the Agency Dashboard**

  


The **Agency Dashboard for Rebilling** provides a centralized view of your agency's rebilling performance, helping you monitor revenue, costs, profits, and usage across all eligible sub-accounts.

  


Use the dashboard to:

  


  * Track rebilling revenue and costs  
  

  * Analyze profit across services  
  

  * Monitor usage trends  
  

  * Export transaction data for reconciliation  
  

  * Filter data by date, product, and sub-account


  


> **Note:** The dashboard displays data for the most recent **six months**. 
> 
> Negative revenue may appear due to **cancellations, refunds, or prorated adjustments**

* * *

## **Before You Begin**

  


Before using the dashboard, keep the following in mind:

  


  * The dashboard displays the most recent six months of data.  
  

  * Metrics are based on your rebilling configuration and available transaction data.  
  

  * Some financial values may differ from external payment processors or exported reports depending on filters, refunds, fees, and transaction timing.

## **Understanding Exported Data and Profit Breakdown**

  


The dashboard shows **aggregated data** , while exports contain **raw transaction-level data**. This difference can make exported files harder to interpret.

###   
**Why Exported Data Feels Generalized**  
  


  * Data is not pre-grouped  
  


  * Profit may not appear as a column  
  


  * Multiple rows may exist per client or service


###   
**Key Fields in Exported Data**  
  


  * Charge (Revenue)  
  


  * Usage Cost (Cost)  
  


  * Location (Sub-account)  
  


  * Service/Product  
  


  * Date


###   
**How to Calculate Profit**  
  


  1. Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets  
  


  2. Locate Charge and Usage Cost  
  


  3. Add a new column: **Profit**  
  


    
    
    Profit = Charge – Usage Cost

###   
**How to Analyze Profit**

  


Use a Pivot Table:  
  


  * Rows → Location  
  


  * Columns → Service  
  


  * Values → Charge and Cost


  


Then calculate profit from totals.

###   
**Important Limitations**

  


  * Profit is not always pre-calculated  
  


  * Data may require grouping  
  


  * Export totals may differ from dashboard if filters differ


  


  


**Metric**| **Recommended Definition**  
---|---  
**Revenue**|  Amount charged to clients for the services they have consumed.  
**Cost**|  Actual charges billed by HighLevel for services consumed by your clients and deducted from your agency wallet.  
**Profit**|  Revenue − Cost

## **Understanding Auto-Recharges vs. Cost and Revenue**

  


Auto-Recharges should not be compared directly with the Cost or Revenue shown in the Agency Dashboard.

  


  * **Cost** represents the actual charges billed by HighLevel for services your clients have used. These charges are deducted from your agency wallet.  
  

  * **Revenue** represents the amount your clients are charged for the services they have consumed.  
  

  * **Auto-Recharges** are funds added to a client's wallet to maintain its balance. They are wallet top-ups and do not represent service usage.


  


Because funds added through an Auto-Recharge may not be used immediately, Auto-Recharge amounts are not expected to match the Cost or Revenue shown in the Agency Dashboard.

  


The Agency Dashboard reflects actual usage-based charges and revenue for the selected reporting period, while wallet funds may be consumed during the current or a future billing period.

## **Troubleshooting**

  


If the data displayed in your Agency Rebilling Dashboard doesn't look as expected, review the following common scenarios.

  


### **1\. My dashboard totals don't match my exported report**

  


The dashboard displays **aggregated financial data** , while exported reports contain **transaction-level records**.

  


Differences can occur if:

  


  * The dashboard and export use different date ranges.  
  

  * Different filters (such as product or sub-account) are applied.  
  

  * Multiple transactions are grouped together in the dashboard but appear as separate rows in the export.  
  


To compare totals accurately, ensure you're using the same filters and date range in both the dashboard and the exported report.

##   


### **2\. My dashboard doesn't match Stripe**

  


The Agency Rebilling Dashboard and Stripe may display different totals because they track different aspects of your transactions.

  


Differences may occur due to:

  


  * Stripe processing fees  
  

  * Refunds processed directly in Stripe  
  

  * Timing differences between transactions and settlements


When comparing data, remember that the Rebilling Dashboard reflects rebilling activity, while Stripe reflects payment processing activity.

##   


### **3\. Why is my revenue negative?**

  


Negative revenue can occur when refunds, cancellations, or prorated adjustments are applied to previously billed transactions.

Review recent account activity and refunds to understand the adjustment reflected in the dashboard.

##   


### **4\. Why doesn't the exported CSV include a Profit column?**

  


The dashboard calculates **Profit** by comparing revenue and cost.

  


Exported reports provide transaction-level data rather than calculated dashboard metrics. If needed, Profit can be calculated by comparing the exported revenue and cost values.

##   


### **5\. I only see six months of data**

  


The Agency Rebilling Dashboard currently displays the most recent **six months** of rebilling data.

This is the current system limitation for dashboard reporting.

##   


### **6\. My profit looks lower than expected**

  


Before reviewing profit calculations, verify that:

  


  * The correct date range is selected.  
  

  * The appropriate product and sub-account filters are applied.  
  

  * The **Include Only Rebilled Transactions** setting matches the data you want to analyze.


  


Changing these filters may affect the revenue, cost, and profit values shown in the dashboard.

##   


### **7\. I'm missing transactions**

  


If expected transactions aren't appearing:

  


  * Verify that the correct filters are selected.  
  

  * Confirm you're viewing the appropriate date range.  
  

  * Review whether the transaction falls within the dashboard's six-month reporting window.  
  

  * Check whether the **Include Only Rebilled Transactions** filter is enabled or disabled, depending on the transactions you're trying to review.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: How is Total Profit calculated?**

Profit = Revenue – Cost

  


  


**Q: Why does my dashboard show negative revenue?**

Due to refunds, cancellations, or adjustments

  


  


**Q: Why don’t numbers match Stripe payouts?**

Stripe fees, timing, and external refunds are not included

  


  


**Q: Why only six months of data?**

This is a system limitation

  


  


**Q: What happens if I disable “Include only rebilled transactions”?**

Non-rebilled costs may be included

  


  


**Q. Why don't Auto-Recharge amounts match the Cost or Revenue in the Agency Dashboard?**

Auto-Recharges add funds to a client's wallet, while the Agency Dashboard reports actual service usage and the associated Cost and Revenue.

  


Funds added through an Auto-Recharge may remain in the client's wallet and be used later. Therefore, Auto-Recharge totals are not expected to match the Cost or Revenue shown in the Agency Dashboard for the same date range.
