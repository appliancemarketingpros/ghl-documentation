# **What is a Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipt?**

  


A Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipt is a downloadable PDF that groups all wallet recharge payments made during a selected month. It helps agencies keep a clear record of funds added to the wallet for billing review, reconciliation, and internal finance tracking.

Each monthly receipt can include:

  


  * Manual Recharges
  * Auto Recharges
  * ACH/Manual Recharges


* * *

## **Key Benefits of Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipts**

  


  * **Monthly tracking:** View all wallet top-up payments for a selected month in one receipt.  
  

  * **Clear separation:** Keep top-up records separate from tax invoices and usage receipts.  
  

  * **Easy reconciliation:** Match monthly wallet funding with internal billing or bookkeeping records.  
  

  * **Downloadable proof:** Save a PDF receipt for payment reference and recordkeeping.


* * *

## **What the Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipt Includes**

  


The receipt is designed to give agencies the key payment details needed to verify wallet funding activity for the month. Each PDF provides a clear record of the recharge entries included in that monthly receipt.

  


The downloadable PDF includes:

  


  * Receipt number
  * Receipt date
  * Billed-to details
  * Issued-by details
  * Itemized recharge line items
  * Payment method
  * Total top-up amount

## **How To Download Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipts**

  


No manual setup is required. Once a monthly receipt is available, you can download it directly from the Billing Dashboard in one simple flow.

  


  1. Login to your Agency account and go to **Settings** > **Billing.**  
  

  2. Click **Wallet & Transactions**.  
  

  3. Open **Invoices**.  
  

  4. Click **Download Receipt** to download the PDF of the **Top-up receipt** for the **particular month**.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Do I need to enable Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipts before using them?**

No. No manual setup is required.

  


**Q: What transactions are included in a Monthly Wallet Top-up Receipt?**  
The receipt can include Manual Recharges, Auto Recharges, and ACH/Manual Recharges made during the selected month.

  


**Q: Is a Top-up Receipt the same as a Tax Invoice?**  
No. A Top-up Receipt is separate from a Tax Invoice and from a Usage Receipt.

  


**Q: Can I get receipts for months before June 2026?**  
Earlier receipts may be generated retrospectively on a case-by-case basis.
