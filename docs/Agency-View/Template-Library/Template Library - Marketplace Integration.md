# **What is Template Library Marketplace Integration?**

  


Template Library Marketplace Integration connects the Template Library with the HighLevel Marketplace so users can discover, preview, install, and sell templates from one place. Instead of relying on separate workflows, users can access Marketplace templates directly inside Template Library and move from browsing to installation more efficiently. This makes it easier to find the right template, evaluate it before use, and manage template selling from within HighLevel.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Template Library Marketplace Integration**

  


  * **Centralized discovery** : Browse Marketplace templates directly from Template Library.  
  


  * **Faster setup** : Install templates without leaving HighLevel.  
  


  * **More template options** : Access both free and paid templates in one place.  
  


  * **Creator monetization** : List your own templates on the Marketplace for sale.  
  


  * **Simplified workflows** : Preview, purchase, install, and manage templates from connected experiences.

## **How to Preview & Install Templates**

  


  1. Go to **Template Library** in **Agency View**.  
  


  2. Click **Marketplace**.  
  


  3. Browse templates or use search and filters.  
  


  4. Open a template **preview**.  
  


  5. **Review** the template **details** and support information.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/HxEsF5CSUvnwPlvHP3cb+/GIF+Recording+2026-07-20+at+20.25.20.gif)  
  


  6. **Select** a **pricing** plan if the template is paid.  
  


  7. Click **Confirm** and complete the flow.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/cKWDgRYTHtBq1kxlFS06+/IeC8b42uyfphaaxZ_mYq07Pc2sCdlXS_CA.jpeg)

## **How to Sell Templates Through the Marketplace**

  
Selling templates through the Marketplace gives creators a direct way to distribute and monetize their work. The built-in selling flow reduces manual setup by sending template details into the Marketplace Seller Portal, where the remaining listing information can be completed.  
  


  1. Login to your Agency Account and **click** on **Template Library**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/uqo4KLlwqx8uE58chBwC+/Screen+Shot+2026-06-29+at+19.54.09.png)  
  


  2. Click **\+ Create Template** to create a template.  
  


  3. **Select** the **sub** -**account** to create from.  
  


  4. Select a **product** **type**.

  


  5. Choose the **template** and click **C****ontinue**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/EIzOn2qOypMHTZt5qOpc+/GIF+Recording+2026-06-29+at+20.07.15.gif)

  
  


  6. **Rename** your **template** and **click** on **create**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/H5EhN9CsaWoH2Zp5ihAE+/Screen+Shot+2026-06-29+at+20.13.38.png)  
  


  7. **Edit** **Template** **Info** and click on **Save**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/bZOOYMiGa4CzUYnThGHo+/Screen+Shot+2026-06-29+at+20.16.58.png)  
  


  8. Click the **three-dot menu** in the template preview.  
  


  9. Select **Sell Template On The Marketplace**.  
  


  10. In the confirmation modal, click **Go to Seller Portal**.  
  


  11. Complete the required Marketplace listing details.  
  


  12. **Submit** the template for review.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/VukgOIVXvreP7gk4gMK1+/GIF+Recording+2026-07-20+at+20.19.23.gif)

## **Marketplace Seller Portal Configuration**

  


Completing the Seller Portal setup ensures your template listing includes the information buyers need before purchasing or installing. Accurate listing details, support details, and pricing help reduce confusion and improve the buyer experience.  
  


### **Template Listing Details**

  


Template listing details help buyers understand what the template includes and how it should be used. Well-prepared listing information also improves discoverability inside the Marketplace.

  
Add or review the following information in the Seller Portal:

  


  * Template link

  * Template name

  * About information

  * Prerequisites

  * Template description

  * Product type

  * Template category

  * Tags

  * Template types

  * Customization details

  * Cover image


  


**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155074516930/original/X0q3posEYtBcMZ6OxCTExs3oX9P8plai7g.png?1782403675)**  
  


### **Support Details**

  
Support information helps buyers understand how to contact the seller and what kind of assistance is available after installation or purchase. Clear support details build trust and reduce post-purchase friction.

  
Add or review the following support information:

  


  * Support email

  * Documentation URL

  * Support website URL

  * Support phone number

  * Terms and Conditions URL

  * Privacy Policy URL

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Is the payment for templates one-time or recurring?**  
Templates are available on a one-time payment basis. Once purchased, you can use the template without any recurring charges.

  


**Q: What happens after I install a template?****  
**The template will be available under My Templates and can be edited and used in builders.

  


**Q: Can I update a template after listing it?**  
Yes, you can refresh your template and share updates with users via the What’s New section.

  


**Q: Can I install both free and paid templates from Template Library?**  
Yes. Users can browse free and paid templates from the **Marketplace** and complete the required install or purchase flow.

  


**Q: Can I filter Marketplace templates before installing them?**

Yes. Available filters can help narrow templates by items such as pricing and install status.  
  


**Q: Where do installed templates appear after installation?**  
Installed templates can be accessed from **My Templates** in the relevant builder.

  


**Q: What information do I need before submitting a template for review?**

You should complete the required listing details, support details, and pricing details if the template is paid.

  


**Q: Can buyers view seller support information before installing a template?**  
Yes. Support details may be visible from the template details experience.
