# SaaS Multi-Brands Overview

The **SaaS Multi-Brands** feature allows agencies to manage **up to two distinct brands** within a single SaaS account. Each brand can have its own independent branding and white-label configurations, which can then be mapped to subaccounts for a consistent and tailored experience.Each brand can have its own logo, domain and WL settings.

## Why Use Multi-Brands?

Using multiple brands gives you:

  * **Flexibility to explore new niches**  
Run different business models or industries under separate brand identities.

  * **A tailored client experience**  
Customize branding and presentation for different sets of clients.


* * *

## What Configurations Make Up a Brand?

The following White Label configurations together define a **brand** :

  * Logo

  * Brand Name

  * Brand Email

  * Brand Phone Number

  * White Label (WL) Domain

  * API Domain

  * Privacy Policy & Terms and Conditions URLs

  * Custom JavaScript

  * Custom CSS

  * Theme


* * *

## Availability
    
    
    **This feature is available only on the $497 Pro plan.**

## Brand Settings

### Mark a Brand as Default

You can mark **one brand as the default** by:

  1. Toggling to the required brand.

  2. Clicking the **three-dot (⋮) menu**.

  3. Selecting **Set as Default**.


  * By default, the **first brand created** is automatically marked as the default brand.

# Attaching Brands to Sub-Accounts

You can associate a brand with a sub-account in two ways:

###   


### 1\. Link Individual Sub-Accounts to a Brand

**During sub-account creation**  
If your agency has multiple brands configured, you’ll see an option to select a brand at the top of the **Create Sub-Account** form.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062424777/original/Gs2OgwvbhIJTTVGvmkebas4RmqBAXS5bRg.png?1768208979)

  


**For existing sub-accounts**  
You can change the linked brand at any time by following these steps:

  1. Go to **Agency Level > Sub-Accounts**

  2. Select the required sub-account

  3. Click **Manage Client**

  4. Click the **brand label** (near the account title)

  5. Select an alternate brand and click **Save**


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062424974/original/GtA32gg9xN09JehyImtF-4q4AkwJbT5ElQ.png?1768209105)

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062425020/original/M3f6bO2_BwYGulm9sx1UMn-tx8rHUuuc-Q.png?1768209136)

  


Once saved, the sub-account will immediately adopt the selected brand’s white-label configurations across all applicable areas.

  

    
    
    **Important**
    When a sub-account’s brand is changed:
    - Users must log in using the domain configured for the new brand.
    - The previous brand’s domain will no longer be accessible for that sub-account.

###   


### 2\. Map a Brand to a SaaS Plan Category

Brands can also be associated with **SaaS plan categories**. When a sub-account purchases a SaaS plan under a specific category, the brand linked to that category is automatically applied to the sub-account.

  


To link a brand to a SaaS plan category:

  1. Go to **SaaS Configurator > Advanced Settings**

  2. Under **Category Management** , click **Edit** for the desired category

  3. Select the brand from the **Select Brand** dropdown

  4. Click **Save**


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062425087/original/364aKycFGKpMAUf0UCOU0IueUhCz1dpj3A.png?1768209180)

You can also select a brand while creating a new SaaS plan category.

  

    
    
    **Note**
    By default, all SaaS plans and categories are linked to the **default brand** configured in Agency Settings.

## **FAQs**

###  1\. Why is the "Create New Brand" CTA greyed out even though I’m on the $497 plan?

The Create New Brand CTA is disabled if a White Label (WL) domain is not configured for your current brand.

The WL domain acts as a unique identifier for each brand. Once you configure a WL domain from Settings → Company → White Label, the Create New Brand option will be enabled, allowing you to proceed with brand creation.

* * *

### 2\. What happens if I delete a brand that already has subaccounts mapped to it?

If a brand with mapped subaccounts is deleted, all associated subaccounts will automatically fall back to the default brand.

This ensures uninterrupted branding and prevents subaccounts from being left without a valid brand configuration.

* * *

### 3\. Where is the default brand used?

The default brand is used in the following scenarios:

  * Subaccounts with no brand attached

  * Newly created subaccounts where no brand is selected during creation

  * Agency-level views and configurations, including agency White Label settings


The default brand acts as a fallback to maintain consistent branding across the platform.
