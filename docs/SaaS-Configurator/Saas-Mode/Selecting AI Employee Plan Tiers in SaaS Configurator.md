Choose Growth or Unlimited AI Employee tiers when building SaaS plans, with clear pricing and feature comparison to ensure clients get the tier you intend.

This article covers how the tier picker works, what agencies see when configuring plans, and how the selected tier carries through to client provisioning.

## What is AI Employee Tier Selection?

AI Employee Tier Selection allows agencies to choose which AI Employee plan tier — Growth or Unlimited — to include when bundling AI Employee into a SaaS plan. Instead of a simple on/off toggle, the SaaS Configurator provides a plan picker that displays each tier's pricing and features side by side.

Once you select a tier, that choice is saved with the plan and displayed in the Configurator's main view. When a client is provisioned on that plan, they receive the AI Employee tier you configured, ensuring alignment between your pricing strategy and what the client actually receives.

This tier-selection approach is built to extend to other multi-tier addons in the future, establishing a consistent pattern for configuring tiered services within SaaS plans.

## Key Benefits

Selecting a specific AI Employee tier when configuring SaaS plans provides clarity and control over pricing, provisioning, and client expectations.

**Accurate Margin Planning** — Build SaaS pricing with confidence by knowing exactly which AI Employee tier cost is included in your plan.

**Correct Client Provisioning** — Clients land on the AI Employee tier you configured, eliminating tier mismatches and billing confusion.

**Clear Pricing Visibility** — See the selected tier and its cost directly in the Configurator's main view without reopening the addon flow.

**Informed Tier Selection** — Compare Growth and Unlimited pricing and features side by side before committing to a tier.

**Reduced Support Escalations** — Fewer ambiguous addon configurations mean fewer tickets from tier-related billing issues.

## How the Plan Picker Works

When you add AI Employee to a SaaS plan, the Configurator displays a plan picker that presents Growth and Unlimited tiers using the same selection pattern familiar from marketplace app plans.

Component 1

Tier Comparison View

Each tier is displayed with its price and a feature list, allowing you to evaluate which tier fits your client's needs and your pricing strategy before making a selection.

Component 2

Persistent Tier Display

Once you select a tier, the Configurator's main screen shows the chosen tier and its cost at a glance, so you don't need to reopen the addon flow to confirm which tier is attached to a plan.

Component 3

Provisioning Integration

The selected tier is saved with the plan configuration and used during client provisioning, ensuring clients receive the exact AI Employee tier you configured rather than a default.

## How to Select an AI Employee Tier in Your SaaS Plan

Follow these steps to add AI Employee to a SaaS plan and choose the appropriate tier for your clients.

Step 1

Open the SaaS Configurator

Navigate to the SaaS Configurator in your agency account and select the plan you want to configure.

Step 2

Add AI Employee to the Plan

Click the option to add AI Employee as an addon. The plan picker will appear, displaying Growth and Unlimited tiers.

Step 3

Review Tier Pricing and Features

Compare the pricing and feature lists for Growth and Unlimited to determine which tier aligns with your client's needs and your pricing model.

Step 4

Select a Tier

Click on your chosen tier (Growth or Unlimited). The picker will save your selection and return you to the Configurator's main view.

## Important Notes and Limitations

Be aware of these considerations when working with AI Employee tier selection in SaaS plans.

Plans Created Before This Feature

If a plan had AI Employee enabled before this feature existed, it may not yet have an explicit tier attached. In some cases, the plan picker may show AI Employee as unselected or priced at $0 until you open the plan and confirm the tier. A default AI Employee tier is applied automatically for plans without one, but you should still verify the tier is correct for your pricing.

Support and Customer Success

Reports of "AI Employee shows as missing or $0 on an existing plan" should be treated as expected transition-period issues. The fix is to reopen the plan and reselect the correct tier.

Pricing Comparison Scope

The pricing and feature comparison displayed in the picker is specific to AI Employee. A more dynamic, backend-driven version for other future addons is a known follow-up, not available for other addons yet.

Current Scope

This release is scoped to AI Employee, though the picker pattern is designed for reuse if other multi-tier addons are introduced later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if my plan had AI Employee enabled before this feature existed?

Plans configured before this release may not have an explicit tier assigned. In some cases, AI Employee may appear unselected or priced at $0 in the Configurator. To resolve this, open the plan in the Configurator and reselect the correct tier (Growth or Unlimited). A default tier may be applied automatically, but you should confirm it matches your pricing strategy.

Q: Can I change the AI Employee tier on an existing plan?

Yes. Open the plan in the SaaS Configurator, access the AI Employee addon settings, and select a different tier from the plan picker. The new tier selection will apply to future client provisioning on that plan.

Q: Does the selected tier affect clients already provisioned on a plan?

Tier changes in the Configurator apply to future client provisioning. Clients already provisioned on a plan retain their current AI Employee tier unless manually updated.

Q: Where can I see which AI Employee tier is attached to a plan?

The SaaS Configurator's main view displays the selected AI Employee tier and its price alongside other plan addons and features. You do not need to reopen the addon flow to confirm the tier.

Q: Will this tier selection pattern be available for other addons?

The tier picker pattern is designed to extend to other multi-tier addons in the future. Currently, it is available only for AI Employee.

Q: What if a plan shows AI Employee at $0 or unselected?

This typically indicates the plan was created before tier selection was available and does not yet have an explicit tier assigned. Open the plan in the Configurator and select the correct tier. Once saved, the tier and its price will display correctly.

Q: Can I compare Growth and Unlimited features before selecting a tier?

Yes. The plan picker displays pricing and features for both Growth and Unlimited tiers side by side, allowing you to evaluate which tier fits your client's needs and your pricing strategy before making a selection.

Q: What should I do if I see a report from an agency about AI Employee showing as $0?

Treat this as an expected transition-period issue for plans created before tier selection existed. Advise the agency to open the plan in the SaaS Configurator and reselect the correct AI Employee tier. Once saved, the issue will be resolved.
