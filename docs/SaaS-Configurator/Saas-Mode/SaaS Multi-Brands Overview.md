## What is SaaS Multi-Brands?

SaaS Multi-Brands enables agencies to manage up to two independent brand identities within a single HighLevel SaaS account. Each brand operates with its own white label configuration, allowing agencies to serve different markets, customer segments, or business verticals under distinct brand identities—all from one agency account.

Each brand can be customized with unique branding elements, mapped to specific sub-accounts, or assigned automatically through SaaS plan categories. This eliminates the need to manage separate agency accounts for different brand identities.

Brands can be switched independently, allowing you to manage each brand's settings, configurations, and sub-account assignments from a centralized interface.

2

## Key Benefits

SaaS Multi-Brands provides agencies with the flexibility to operate multiple brand identities without the overhead of managing separate accounts.

**Serve Multiple Markets** — Operate distinct brands for different customer segments, geographic regions, or business verticals under one agency account.

**Explore New Niches** — Use a second brand to test new markets or service offerings while maintaining your primary brand identity.

**Simplified Account Management** — Manage multiple brands from a single agency dashboard without maintaining separate agency accounts or login credentials.

**Tailored Client Experience** — Deliver a fully branded experience with unique logos, domains, themes, and contact information for each brand.

**Flexible Brand Assignment** — Map brands to individual sub-accounts or automate brand assignment through SaaS plan categories.

## Brand Configuration Capabilities

Each brand in SaaS Multi-Brands can be configured independently with the following white label settings and branding elements.

Feature 1

Brand Name and Logo

Define a unique brand name and upload a custom logo for each brand to ensure consistent branding across all customer touchpoints.

Feature 2

White Label Domain

Configure a unique white label domain for each brand, allowing customers to access their accounts through a branded URL specific to that brand identity.

Feature 3

API Domain

Set a custom API domain for each brand to ensure API integrations and webhooks align with your brand identity.

Feature 4

Brand Email and Phone Number

Specify a unique contact email address and phone number for each brand, providing customers with brand-specific support channels.

Feature 5

Theme Customization

Apply a unique theme to each brand, including color schemes, button styles, and layout preferences.

Feature 6

Custom CSS and JavaScript

Inject custom CSS and JavaScript for each brand to further customize the user interface and functionality.

Feature 7

Privacy Policy and Terms URLs

Define custom URLs for privacy policies and terms of service for each brand, ensuring compliance and brand-specific legal documentation.

Note

Each brand operates with its own independent white label configuration. Changes made to one brand do not affect the settings of the other brand.

## How to Set Up SaaS Multi-Brands

Follow these steps to create and configure multiple brands within your SaaS account.

Step 1

Access SaaS Configuration

Navigate to your agency dashboard and access the SaaS Configuration section. This is where you manage all brand settings and configurations.

Step 2

Create a New Brand

Click the option to create a new brand. You can create up to two brands per agency account. Provide a brand name and upload a logo for the new brand.

Step 3

Configure White Label Settings

Set up the white label domain, API domain, brand email, phone number, theme, custom CSS/JavaScript, and privacy/terms URLs for the new brand. Each setting is independent from your other brand.

Step 4

Set a Default Brand

Designate one brand as the default fallback for agency-wide settings. This brand is used when a sub-account or SaaS plan category does not have a specific brand assigned.

Step 5

Map Brands to Sub-Accounts

Assign a brand to individual sub-accounts. When a sub-account is mapped to a brand, customers accessing that sub-account see the branding, domain, and settings associated with the assigned brand.

Step 6

Automate Brand Assignment via SaaS Plan Categories

Link brands to SaaS plan categories to automatically assign branding based on the plan a customer subscribes to. This allows you to deliver different brand experiences depending on the SaaS plan tier without manual assignment.

Step 7

Switch Between Brands

Use the brand switcher in your SaaS Configuration dashboard to toggle between your brands and manage their settings independently. Changes to one brand do not affect the other.

Setup Complete

Your SaaS Multi-Brands setup is complete. Customers accessing sub-accounts or plans mapped to each brand will see the appropriate branding and white label settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many brands can I create in a single SaaS account?

You can create up to two independent brands per agency account. Each brand operates with its own white label configuration, allowing you to serve different customer segments under distinct brand identities.

Q: Do I need a separate agency account to use multiple brands?

No. SaaS Multi-Brands allows you to manage multiple brand identities from a single agency account. You do not need to maintain separate agency accounts or login credentials.

Q: Can I assign different brands to different sub-accounts?

Yes. You can map brands to individual sub-accounts, ensuring that each sub-account displays the branding, domain, and settings associated with the assigned brand.

Q: Can I automate brand assignment based on SaaS plan categories?

Yes. You can link brands to SaaS plan categories. When a customer subscribes to a plan in a specific category, the associated brand is automatically assigned to their sub-account.

Q: What happens if a sub-account is not assigned to a brand?

If a sub-account is not assigned to a specific brand, the default brand you designated in your SaaS Configuration settings is used as the fallback.

Q: Is SaaS Multi-Brands available during the Private Beta?

Yes. SaaS Multi-Brands is currently available in Private Beta for an initial cohort of 20–30 agencies. Public Beta availability is planned within 2–3 weeks.

Q: What plan do I need to access SaaS Multi-Brands?

SaaS Multi-Brands is available exclusively to agencies on the $497 Pro Plan. This feature is not accessible on other SaaS plan tiers.

Q: Can I use different domains for each brand?

Yes. Each brand can have its own white label domain and API domain, allowing you to deliver a fully branded experience with unique URLs for each brand identity.
