# SaaS Plan Labels Updated to V1 and V2

**Source URL:** [https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007778-saas-plan-labels-updated-to-v1-and-v2](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007778-saas-plan-labels-updated-to-v1-and-v2)  
**Category:** SaaS Configurator  
**Folder:** Saas Mode

---

To reduce confusion between different SaaS plans, HighLevel has updated plan labels across the platform.  
  


  * **Agency Plan** is now labeled **SaaS V1 Plan**

  * **SaaS Plan** is now labeled **SaaS V2 Plan**


  


This is a label-only change. There are no updates to pricing, features, billing, or functionality.

## **What’s Changed**

  


The naming of SaaS plans has been updated to clearly distinguish between legacy and newer plan structures:  
  


  * **SaaS V1 Plan** → Previously known as _Agency Plan_  
  


  * **SaaS V2 Plan** → Previously known as _SaaS Plan_


  


These updates apply automatically to all existing and new products.

* * *

## **Where You’ll See This**

  


You will see the updated labels in:  
  


  * **Payments → Products**  
  


  * SaaS product listings across the platform

## **Do You Need to Take Action?**

  


No action is required.  
  


  * Your existing products remain unchanged  
  


  * Pricing and billing are unaffected  
  


  * Subscriptions continue as normal


* * *

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Does this change affect pricing or billing?**

No. This is a label-only update. Pricing and billing remain the same.  
  


**Q: Do I need to update or recreate my plans?**

No. All plans are automatically updated with the new labels.  
  


**Q: Are SaaS V1 and SaaS V2 different products now?**

No. These are the same plans as before, with updated naming for clarity.  
  


**Q: Will my clients see any changes?**

Only the plan name may appear differently. There are no changes to billing or functionality.  
  


**Q: Why was this change made?**

The update helps clearly distinguish between legacy (V1) and newer (V2) SaaS plan structures.
