# **Overview of Purchasing a Domain**

  


Domain Purchase allows eligible HighLevel agencies and sub-accounts to search for and register a new domain directly inside HighLevel. Instead of purchasing a domain from a third-party registrar and then connecting it separately, users can complete the registration process from within their HighLevel account and manage the domain alongside the products and sites that use it.

  


This makes it easier to move from purchasing a domain to using it with supported HighLevel products, including websites, funnels, stores, and other digital assets that require a connected domain.

  


HighLevel also supports multi-year registration for eligible TLDs during checkout. This gives users the option to secure a domain for a longer period upfront, helping reduce the need for annual renewals while providing more control over long-term domain ownership.

  

    
    
    **Note:** Your domain automatically comes with WHOIS privacy protection, ensuring your personal details are not visible in public lookups.

**  
**
    
    
    **Important:** If your account has been cancelled and you need to request the release or transfer of a purchased domain, submit the [domain release request form here](<https://www.gohighlevel.com/domain-transfer>).

## **Key Benefits of Purchasing a Domain in HighLevel**

  


  * **Centralized domain management:** Purchase and manage registered domains from the same HighLevel account.  
  

  * **Multi-year registration:** Register eligible domains for multiple years during checkout instead of renewing every year.  
  

  * **Pricing transparency:** Review the price for the selected domain and registration term before completing the purchase.  
  

  * **Simplified setup:** After purchase, configure the domain or connect it to supported HighLevel products from the Purchased Domains area.

## **Multi-Year Domain Registration**

  


The multi-year domain purchase feature allows you to register a new domain for more than one year — up to 10 years (depending on the TLD). Instead of renewing your domain annually, you can choose to secure it for multiple years during the initial checkout process.  
  


This feature is designed to help businesses and individuals manage their domains more proactively and ensure brand consistency without the hassle of yearly renewals.

  


To ensure you make the most of this feature, keep the following points in mind:  
  


  * Most TLDs (Top-Level Domains) can be registered for up to 10 years.  
  

  * The .co family of TLDs (.co, .com.co, .net.co, .nom.co) supports up to 5 years of registration.  
  

  * If the renewal price is different from the initial registration price for a given TLD, the domain can only be registered for 1 year at the time of purchase.

## **How to Enable Domain Purchase**

  


Domain purchasing must be enabled before sub-accounts can register domains through HighLevel. Agencies control this setting at the agency level, helping determine whether clients can access the built-in domain purchase experience.

  


To enable Domain Purchase:

  


  1. From the **A****gency View,** navigate to **Settings** > **Company** and enable the **Domain Purchase** option. Review and accept the applicable Domain Purchase terms and confirm the change.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078065460/original/JdiLmKawD77YOTwH1_CWaKYsfIXk1PrbRg.png?1786385953)  

  2. You can enable or disable the Domain Purchase feature or rebill for each Sub-Account in **Agency Settings > Domain Purchase**. You can access Sub-Account Settings only if Domain Purchase is enabled at the Agency Level.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078065510/original/YiHJ-qZKi1VSf_jBM7r_nju8gCeEhwh0KQ.png?1786386044)

## **How to Purchase a Domain**

  


Purchasing a domain involves searching for an available name, reviewing the registration options, and confirming the purchase. Carefully reviewing the domain name, registration period, and total cost before checkout is important because domain registrations are finalized once the purchase is completed.

  

    
    
    **Important:** If your account has been cancelled and you need to request the release or transfer of a domain purchased through HighLevel, please submit the domain release request form [here](<https://www.gohighlevel.com/domain-transfer>).

  


#### _**Step 1:** Open Domains_

  


From the applicable sub-account, go to **Settings** > **Domains & URL Redirects**. Click on **Purchase/Transfer Domain**

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078066197/original/GUWWaz7r8cfGsnQAUJCALhsTX0e72PQ18g.png?1786386934)

  


  


####  _**Step 2:** Search for a Domain_

  


Enter the domain name you want to purchase. It will display whether the requested domain is available and may provide alternative domain suggestions. 

  


Locate the domain you want and click **Buy**.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078066526/original/O5O8bTmzfWBlNRA_wCVoWYI34AsQf372Hw.png?1786387289)

  


  


#### _**Step 3:** Select a Registration Period_

  


Open the **Pricing Option** dropdown for the primary domain.

  


Choose the number of years you want to register the domain for from the available options. Registration periods can be up to 10 years but vary based on the TLD. The displayed price updates based on the selected term.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078066629/original/WuWuG9NjBkJnrQ5rF1l7JRkk-DHQHdDKBg.png?1786387479)

  


  


#### _**Step 4:** Add Additional Domain Extensions_

  


After you select the registration period for your main domain, you will see **Protect Your Brand From Competitors — Grab These Extensions Too!** with additional options such as `.org`, `.net`, and `.uk`. Select any extensions you want to add.

  


Each additional domain is added with a 1-year registration period by default. To change the registration period for an added domain, click its price and select the number of years you want. You can set a different registration period for each domain or remove an added domain using the **delete** icon.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078066957/original/JHUrhIhXx6-rF0kuArCoRpTTCwkemihgUA.png?1786387923)

  


  


#### **_Step 5:_**_Complete the Purchase_

  


When you're ready, agree to the **Terms and Conditions** and click **Pay**. It takes about 1 minute to complete the domain registration process.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078066988/original/OA1WqQ0WNErrkMCnrsa9QHj-xRYiJ0mKyA.png?1786387992)

## **Start a domain purchase from Funnels, Websites, Stores, or Webinars**

  


You can also start a domain purchase directly from the asset you are working on.

  


In a Funnel, Website, Store, or Webinar Settings screen, click **Purchase Domain** to open the domain purchase page and start a domain search.  
  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155061424052/original/umPqqKA0sukqs5CfwL1SbBIz4syWkM7xmw.png?1766857998)

  


  


You will see similar options inside the builder:

  


  1. In the Funnel, Website, Store, or Webinar builder, click **Connect Domain.**  
  

  2. Choose **Purchase Domain** when prompted. This opens the same domain purchase page in a new tab where you can search for and purchase a new domain.


  


After purchasing your domain, continue with the configuration and connection steps in this article to use it with your funnels, websites, stores, or webinars.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155061424050/original/jQgtLMA7NpCzBrbOuAJy3taSirDS_e3-5g.png?1766857965)

## **After Purchasing Your Domain**

  


Once the domain registration is complete, the purchased domain appears under **Settings > Domains > Purchased Domains**.

  


To configure DNS records or connect the domain to a funnel, website, store, blog, or other supported product, see [Configure & Connect Purchased Domains.](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000004674>)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: What happens if my account is cancelled but I still need access to a domain I purchased for multiple years?******  
**If your account has been cancelled and you need to request the release or transfer of a purchased domain, submit the[domain release request form here](<https://www.gohighlevel.com/domain-transfer>).**

  


**Q: Why can I only register some domains for 1 year?**  
Some TLDs do not support longer initial registration periods. If the renewal price differs from the registration price, the domain can only be registered for 1 year at purchase.

  


**Q: Can I extend my domain registration later?**  
Yes. You can renew or extend the domain later based on the options available for that TLD at the time of renewal.

  


**Q: Will I receive reminders before my multi-year registration ends?**  
Yes. You’ll still receive renewal reminders before your domain registration period ends to ensure you have the option to renew in advance.

**Q: Is WHOIS privacy included with domains purchased through HighLevel?**  
Yes. WHOIS privacy protection is automatically included so your personal registration details are not visible in public WHOIS lookups.

  


**Q: Does purchasing for multiple years change my renewal price?**  
No. Your upfront payment covers the total cost of the selected registration period. Renewal pricing in future years will follow standard rates at that time.
