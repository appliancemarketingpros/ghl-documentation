## What are Rentals?

Rentals is a scheduling and booking system designed for businesses that manage rentals — whether it's properties, cars, equipment, or other bookable assets. With Rentals, you can efficiently manage availability, inventory, pricing, and multi-day bookings in one seamless platform.

## Feature of Rentals

Rentals is built to handle the unique complexities of rental-based scheduling. Here's what Rentals can do:

**Multi-Day Booking Support** — Book assets across multiple hours, days, weeks, or months, with flexible duration and booking controls.

**Listings and Categories** — Create listings for your rentals with detailed information, organized categories, and status management. **[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573>)[**(Help Guide)**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573>)[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573>)**

**Inventory & Variants** — Enable quantity tracking for multiple units and define variants for different versions of the same listing. **[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579>)[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/a/solutions/articles/155000006579?portalId=48000045315#Inventory-&-Pricing>)[(Help Guide)](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006579-create-edit-listings#Inventory-&-Pricing>)**

**Advanced Pricing & Discounts** — Use pricing rules for seasons, days of the week, hour of day, durations, or quantities. Automatically apply discounts for long-term or bulk bookings. **[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611>)[**(Help Guide)**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611>)[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611>)**

**Calendar View** — View all bookings visually using a modern, filterable calendar that supports day, week, and month views. Identify overlaps, blocked slots, and real-time availability. **[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641>)[**(Help Guide)**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641>)[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641>)**[](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641>)**

**External Calendar Sync** — Connect and sync bookings with third party platforms using Calendar Sync to ensure your availability is consistent across platforms.

**Centralized Global Settings** — Control business operating hours, booking defaults, payment preferences, and communication templates from one location. **(****[Help Guide](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006640>))**

**Built-in Payments and Invoicing** — Collect payments directly from the booking flow and generate invoices automatically for each confirmed booking. Set up security deposits per your business needs.

**Customer Booking Page** — Offer customers a streamlined booking experience with a dedicated public-facing booking page that displays your listings, categories, pricing and availability in real-time.

## Setting Up Rentals

If you don't see the Rentals tab in your Calendar settings, enable it by following the instructions in [**How To Enable/Disable Rentals For Your Locations**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006740>).

**Note**

Rental invoices are not automatically sent to the customer whenever a booking is created. We are working on rectifying the information being displayed within the contacts/conversations page that conveys otherwise.

  


Once Rentals is enabled for your sub-account, continue:

Step 1

Access Rentals

Go to **Calendars** → **Calendar** **Settings** → **Rentals.**

  


**![](https://jumpshare.com/share/baSRelYN0BEmC0nRWaTK+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-05+at+18.51.55.gif)**

Step 2

Create Listing

  


  1. Click **\+ New Listing** to add your first rental item or property.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/53tYqVgL3g7VQ5ZRtoWl+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-05+at+18.54.01.png)  

  2. **Configure** its **image** , **name** , **description** , **price** , and **availability**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/tfSZVdn6xiV1NfdQw6DG+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-05+at+19.06.07.gif)  

  3. Use **Advanced** **Settings** to **define** **inventory** , **variants** , **pricing** , and **booking** **rules**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/DnwjpvHZAvK08bOtk6rt+/Screen+Shot+2026-08-05+at+19.17.30.png)  


To get more information on how to create a listing, read our article on [**Creating and Editing Listings**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579>).

Step 3

Customize Global Settings

Navigate to **Rentals** → **Global** **Settings** to adjust **business** **hours** , **communication** **settings** , and **payment** **methods**.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/eiXb8LeYF1knNnwTNTtY+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-05+at+19.20.04.gif)  


For more information on how to customize global settings, read our article on [**Global Settings in Rentals.** ](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006640>)

Step 4

Test a Booking

**Create** a **test** **booking** from the **Calendar** **View** or **Appointments** List View to verify configuration, pricing, and notifications.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/LEhBlbirXieZ5dudVG59+/GIF+Recording+2026-08-05+at+19.24.16.gif)  


Looking for more information on how to create a booking? Read our article on [**Creating a Booking in Rentals**](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006624>)

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do Calendar and Appointments differ?

Use Calendar View for a visual schedule and overlaps; use Appointments List View for per-listing rows, payments, and quick actions.

Q: What's the difference between Listings and Categories?

A listing is a rentable item. A category groups listings and influences how they're organized on the booking page.

Q: Do global changes affect existing bookings?

Most settings apply to new bookings going forward. Existing bookings and issued invoices typically remain unchanged.

Q: Where do I edit pricing if totals look off?

Adjust rules on the listing's Pricing tab (see Pricing & Discounts) and re-test.

Q: How do external calendars affect availability?

Imported events block time; exports share your availability. Use Refresh in a listing's Calendar Sync to force an update.
