## What is Calendar Setup Assistant?

Calendar Setup Assistant is an AI-powered setup experience inside HighLevel Calendars that helps you create a booking calendar by answering simple questions. Instead of choosing a calendar type and configuring settings manually, you describe your scheduling needs in natural language or select suggested options, and the assistant builds the calendar for you.

The assistant guides you through key setup details such as calendar purpose, calendar name, team members, assignment logic, meeting location, availability, and related booking settings. This conversational approach reduces setup confusion and helps you reach a working calendar faster.

Once the calendar is created, you can review the generated booking link, preview the booking page, book a test appointment, or open calendar settings for additional changes. Calendar Setup Assistant may only be available to eligible users or accounts during rollout.

2

## Key Benefits

Calendar Setup Assistant helps you move from setup to booking faster by removing rigid upfront decisions and guiding you through the right configuration questions. This is especially useful when creating your first calendar or setting up a common booking use case.

**Faster Calendar Creation:** Create a working calendar through a guided conversation instead of configuring every setting manually.

**Simpler Setup Experience:** Describe your use case in plain language or select suggested options to get started.

**Contextual Guidance:** Answer only the questions needed for your calendar setup.

**Booking Page Preview:** Review how the booking page looks while the calendar is being configured.

**Test Booking Option:** Book a test appointment after setup to confirm the customer experience.

**Easy Manual Editing:** Open calendar settings after creation to fine-tune availability, team members, locations, and other settings.

## Where to Find Calendar Setup Assistant

Calendar Setup Assistant appears inside the Calendars area for eligible users. It opens as a side panel so you can create or learn about calendars while staying in the Calendar workspace.

To find Calendar Setup Assistant:

Step 1

Go to **Calendars** in HighLevel.

Step 2

Open the calendar workspace.

Step 3

Look for the **Calendar Setup Assistant** panel and choose one of the available options:

  * **Create a new calendar**
  * **Discover what calendars can do**


Note

If the assistant is not visible, the account may not be eligible during the current rollout, or you may not have the required calendar permissions.

## Starting a Calendar Setup Conversation

Calendar Setup Assistant lets you begin with either a guided option or a natural language request. This makes setup more flexible for users who know exactly what they want and users who need help choosing the right calendar structure.

You can start by:

Clicking **Create a new calendar**

Clicking **Discover what calendars can do**

Selecting a suggested use case chip

Typing a request in your own words

Example requests:

  * "Set up a calendar for onboarding calls."
  * "Create a calendar for me and my colleague."
  * "I need a class booking calendar."
  * "Help me create a 1:1 appointment calendar."

## Guided Calendar Setup Questions

The assistant asks contextual questions based on the calendar use case. This helps gather only the information needed to create a usable calendar instead of requiring you to understand every calendar setting upfront.

The assistant may ask for details such as:

Calendar purpose

Calendar name

Team members

Booking assignment logic

Meeting location

Available days and hours

Booking duration

Buffer settings

Date range

Other related calendar settings

For team calendars, the assistant may ask how bookings should be assigned, such as auto-assigning to available team members or allowing clients to choose their host.

Appointment duration

Selected date

Timezone

Available time slots

Booking page layout

## Creating the Calendar

Once the assistant has enough information, it summarizes the suggested calendar setup for review. This gives you a chance to confirm key details before creating the calendar.

The summary may include:

Calendar name

Appointment duration

Meeting location

Team members

Availability

Assignment logic

After reviewing the suggested setup, you can create the calendar or request changes if the details need adjustment.

## Booking a Test Appointment

After the calendar is created, you can book a test appointment to confirm that the booking flow works as expected. This helps you experience the calendar the same way a customer or client would.

Use the test booking option to confirm:

The booking page opens correctly

Available time slots appear as expected

The booking can be completed

The appointment appears in the calendar

Any connected calendar or meeting location behaves as expected

## Editing the Calendar After Setup

Calendars created through Calendar Setup Assistant can be reviewed and adjusted in calendar settings. This is useful when you want to fine-tune settings after the assistant creates the initial calendar.

After creation, you can open calendar settings to adjust:

Calendar name

Description

Availability

Team members

Meeting location

Booking duration

Buffers

Date range

Notifications

Custom booking page settings

Use **Go to calendar settings** when available, or open the calendar manually from the Calendars settings area.

## Permissions and Access

Calendar Setup Assistant respects existing HighLevel calendar permissions. You must have the required permissions to create or manage calendars.

If you cannot create a calendar through the assistant, check:

The user's calendar permissions

The user's role and access level

Whether the account is eligible for Calendar Setup Assistant

Whether the user has access to the Calendars area

## How to Create a Calendar with Calendar Setup Assistant

A guided setup helps create a working calendar faster while still giving you control over key details. Start by describing what the calendar is for, then answer the assistant's follow-up questions.

Step 1

Go to **Calendars** in HighLevel.

Step 2

Open **Calendar Setup Assistant**.

Step 3

Click **Create a new calendar**.

Step 4

Select a suggested use case or type what you need.

Step 5

Answer the assistant's setup questions.

Step 6

Add team members when prompted, if needed.

Step 7

Choose booking assignment logic, if applicable.

Step 8

Select a meeting location.

Step 9

Set available days and hours.

Step 10

Review the suggested calendar setup.

Step 11

Click **Create it** when the setup looks correct.

Step 12

Review the generated booking link.

Step 13

Click **Book a test appointment** to validate the booking flow.

Step 14

Click **Go to calendar settings** if you need to make additional changes.

Success

Your calendar is now fully functional and appears in Calendar Settings with all standard configuration options available for manual adjustment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is Calendar Setup Assistant?

Calendar Setup Assistant is a guided setup experience that helps eligible users create a booking calendar through conversation.

Q: Where do I find Calendar Setup Assistant?

Calendar Setup Assistant appears inside the Calendars area for eligible users. If you do not see it, your account may not currently have access or your user role may not have the required calendar permissions.

Q: Can I type my own request instead of using suggested options?

Yes. You can describe your calendar use case in your own words or select one of the suggested options.

Q: Can I edit a calendar after the assistant creates it?

Yes. Calendars created with Calendar Setup Assistant can be edited from Calendar Settings like any other calendar.

Q: Can I book a test appointment after setup?

Yes, when available, use the test booking option to confirm the booking flow from the customer’s perspective.

Q: Does Calendar Setup Assistant replace manual calendar setup?

No. Calendar Setup Assistant provides a guided setup option, but you can still create and edit calendars manually.

Q: Why do I not see Calendar Setup Assistant?

Calendar Setup Assistant may only be available to eligible users or accounts during rollout. You may also need the correct calendar permissions.
