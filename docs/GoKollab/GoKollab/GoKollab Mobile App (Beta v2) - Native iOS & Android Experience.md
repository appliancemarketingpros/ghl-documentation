## What is Kollab Mobile App?

Kollab is HighLevel's native mobile application for Communities, Courses, Events, and Chats. Re-engineered from the ground up, Beta v2 replaces the legacy hybrid build with a fully native experience that launches in under two seconds, streams media through HighLevel's Media Core platform, and delivers real-time notifications you can trust.

Available for both iOS and Android, the app provides seamless access to your learning and community spaces wherever you are, with performance and stability that match the best native mobile experiences.

## How to Set Up Kollab Mobile App

A native install ensures you get the best performance and notification reliability. Follow these steps to get started:

Step 1

Download the App

**iOS:** Open the Apple App Store, search for "Kollab by LeadConnector," then tap Download.

**Android:** Open Google Play Store, search for "Kollab by LeadConnector," then tap Install.

Step 2

Sign In with Your Credentials

Use the same email and password (or OTP) you use for your HighLevel account or Community access. If you belong to multiple domains, you'll be prompted to choose the domain you want to enter.

Step 3

Grant Push Notification Permissions

When prompted, allow notifications to receive real-time alerts for chats, posts, comments, and course updates.

Step 4

Fine-Tune Notification Settings (Optional)

Open **Settings › Notifications** inside the app to choose which alert types you want to receive.

Step 5

Explore the Bottom Tabs

Navigate between Courses, Communities, Events, and Chats using the bottom-tab menu to start learning and engaging.

Step 6

Keep the App Updated

Enable auto-updates via the App Store or Play Store to receive the latest performance improvements and feature rollouts.

Success

You're all set! Start engaging with your Communities, Courses, Events, and Chats from your mobile device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I have the old Kollab app—do I need to reinstall?

Yes. Beta v2 is distributed as a separate package. Delete the legacy app and install the latest version from the App Store or Play Store for the best performance.

Q: Do I need a HighLevel account to use the app?

You only need to be invited to at least one Community, Course, or Client Portal. Use the same email and password (or OTP) you use on the web.

Q: Can I disable certain push notifications?

Absolutely. Go to **Profile › Settings › Notifications** and toggle off any alert categories you don't need.

Q: Does Beta v2 work offline?

Limited offline caching is available for previously loaded content, but a live connection is required for new data and media streaming.

Q: When will the Unified Feed and GoLive features ship?

They're on the public roadmap and will roll out in future Beta updates—keep auto-updates enabled to get them as soon as they launch.

Q: Will there be branded (white-label) versions?

Yes. Branded Client Portal apps with the same native codebase are planned, so agencies can offer a custom-label experience.

Q: Is data synced between mobile and web?

Yes. All actions—posts, comments, progress—sync instantly with your web workspace.
