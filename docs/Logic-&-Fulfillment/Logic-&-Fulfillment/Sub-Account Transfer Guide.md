A sub-account transfer moves an entire sub-account from one HighLevel agency, known as the releasing agency, to another HighLevel agency, known as the receiving agency. A transfer does not merge two sub-accounts or move only selected data. Contacts, conversations, supported assets, and account history remain together within the transferred sub-account.

## **Who Can Request or Complete Sub-Account Transfers?**

  


Transfer permissions protect client data by limiting who can move a sub-account between agencies.

By default, only the **Agency Owner** can request or complete a sub-account transfer.

  


The **Agency Owner** can grant sub-account transfer permission to specific **Agency Admins**. Agency Admins without this permission cannot submit or approve transfer requests.

The receiving agency must be active and eligible to accept the additional sub-account. Before starting the transfer, confirm that the receiving agency has the required account capacity, compliance add-ons, and active account status.

  


  

    
    
    **Note: You must be the Agency Owner or an Agency Admin with sub-account transfer permission to perform this action.**

## **Phone Number Transfers**

  

    
    
    **NOTE:**Phone numbers may transfer automatically depending on the phone provider used by both agencies.****

###   


### If Both Agencies Use LC Phone

  * Phone numbers transfer with the sub-account.

  * Eligible A2P registration stays with the numbers.

  * Future phone usage is billed to the receiving agency. 


### If Either Agency Uses Twilio

Phone numbers do not transfer automatically. The agencies and Twilio account owner must coordinate the move separately.

For detailed requirements, see the **Moving Phone Numbers: Migration Guide**.

## **Email Services Transfer**

  


## Email Service Transfers

Email settings may change depending on whether the sub-account and receiving agency use LC Email or another email provider.

### If the Sub-Account Uses LC Email

  * **Agency-assigned dedicated domain:** The domain is removed during the transfer.

  * **Sub-account-owned dedicated domain:** The domain transfers with the sub-account.

  * **Dedicated IP:** The IP is unassigned and the related subscription is removed.

  * **Email verification:** The existing On or Off setting is preserved.

  * **After transfer:** If the receiving agency uses LC Email, the sub-account stays on LC Email. If the receiving agency uses another provider, the sub-account switches to that provider.


### If the Sub-Account Uses Mailgun or SMTP

Existing Mailgun or SMTP configurations are removed.

The sub-account adopts the receiving agency’s default email provider.

  


For additional assistance, contact HighLevel Support.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Can I merge one sub-account with another?**

No. A transfer moves the entire sub-account from one agency to another. It does not combine the transferred account’s data with another sub-account.

  


**Q: Can I transfer only part of a sub-account?**

No. Partial sub-account transfers are not supported. The complete sub-account and its supported data move together.

  


**Q: Can I transfer a HIPAA-enabled sub-account?**

Yes. The receiving agency must have the required HIPAA Compliance add-on before the transfer can be completed.

  


**Q: How long does a sub-account transfer take, and how can I check its status?**

The sub-account transfers after the receiving agency approves the request. Timing may vary if eligibility requirements or account dependencies need to be resolved.

You can monitor the request from **Agency Level > Sub-Accounts > Sub-Account Transfers** to see its current status.

  


**Q: How will I know when the transfer is complete?**

The receiving agency receives an email when the transfer request is submitted. Both agencies can monitor the request from the **Sub-Account Transfers** page.

Review the transfer record to determine whether the request is pending, completed, rejected, canceled, or blocked.
