# HighLevel Mobile App Experience

A ground-up rebuild that transforms your phone into a business command center with lightning-fast navigation, role-aware dashboards, true-black Dark Mode, and an AI assistant.

What You'll Learn

The redesigned HighLevel Mobile App delivers a modern interface, streamlined App Drawer, role-based homepage, Universal Search, and the Ask AI assistant to help you communicate, sell, and analyze faster than ever.

This article covers the key benefits, navigation structure, homepage widgets, AI capabilities, and step-by-step setup instructions for enabling the experience in your agency and subaccounts.

Labs Feature

This feature is available through HighLevel Labs. Enable it in **Agency Settings → Labs → Subaccounts** and select which subaccounts should access the new mobile experience. Test in your agency subaccount before rolling out to clients.

## Key Benefits of the Mobile App

The redesigned mobile experience combines speed, intelligence, and role-aware design to streamline every aspect of business management on the go.

**Unified Command Center** — Critical tasks, messages, revenue, and appointments surface instantly on the homepage, giving you a complete snapshot of your day.

**Faster Workflows** — Universal Search, Quick Actions, and drag-to-reorder Pinned Apps reduce taps and accelerate common tasks.

**Ask AI on the Go** — Draft social posts, update CRM records, and generate images without leaving the chat interface.

**Battery-Saving Dark Mode** — True-black design optimized for OLED screens extends device battery life during extended use.

**SaaS Tier Enforcement** — Features shown match each account's subscription level, maintaining consistency between mobile and web platforms.

## Ask AI

Your AI coworker travels with you, enabling content creation, CRM updates, and image generation directly from the mobile app. Chat History stores every session for easy reference.

**Draft Content** — Generate social posts, emails, and ad copy in seconds without switching apps.

**Create or Edit Records** — Update contacts, opportunities, and other CRM records through conversational commands.

**Generate and Edit Images** — Create branded visuals and modify existing images directly on mobile.

**Chat History** — Access all past AI conversations for reference or continuation of previous sessions.

Note

Ask AI follows your existing AI usage limits and subscription tier. No separate mobile fee applies.

## SaaS Configurator Enforcement

The mobile app enforces SaaS Configurator tiers, hiding modules and features that a user's subscription level does not allow. This maintains perfect consistency between mobile and web platforms, ensuring clients see only the tools included in their plan.

Note

If certain modules are missing from the App Drawer, verify the account's subscription tier and user role permissions in the web dashboard.

Performance, design, and security improvements throughout the app create a smoother, more efficient mobile experience.

## How to Set Up the Mobile App Experience

Enable the redesigned mobile experience in three quick steps.

Step 1

Update the Mobile App

Download the latest version of the HighLevel Mobile App from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Step 2

Enable the Experience in Labs

Navigate to **Agency Settings → Labs → Subaccounts**.

Toggle on **"A New Mobile App Experience"**.

Select the subaccounts where you want to enable the redesigned interface.

Step 3

Explore the App

Open the mobile app on your device and explore the new homepage, Universal Search, App Drawer, and Ask AI features.

Pro Tip

Test the experience first in your agency subaccount before rolling it out to client subaccounts to familiarize yourself with the interface and capabilities.

White-Label Apps

Agencies using white-label mobile app builds should request a new binary after enabling the Labs toggle to ensure the redesigned experience appears in their branded apps.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will my clients automatically see the new design?

No. You control rollout by enabling the toggle per subaccount in Labs. The experience remains opt-in until you activate it for specific subaccounts.

Q: Which operating systems are supported?

Both iOS and Android are supported. Update to the latest store version from the Apple App Store or Google Play to access the redesigned experience.

Q: Can I customize homepage widgets?

Widget customization is not available yet. This capability is planned for Q4 2025 as part of the product roadmap.

Q: Does Ask AI cost extra?

Ask AI follows your existing AI usage limits and subscription tier. No separate mobile fee applies.

Q: Why don't I see certain modules in the App Drawer?

SaaS Configurator and user role permissions determine module visibility. Check the account's subscription tier and user permissions in the web dashboard to verify access.

Q: Is Dark Mode automatic?

The app follows your device's system theme by default. You can also toggle Dark Mode manually in the app's Settings.

Q: Will Universal Search include Payments and Calendars?

Those modules will be added to Universal Search later in 2025 as part of the ongoing "Coming Soon" roadmap.

Q: Do I need to reinstall my white-label mobile app?

Agencies using white-label builds should request a new binary after enabling the Labs toggle to ensure the redesigned experience appears in their branded apps.
