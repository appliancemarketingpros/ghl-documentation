Complete reference guide to campaign use case types required for A2P 10DLC registration

You'll learn which use cases are approved, which require special verification, and which messaging categories are forbidden by carriers.

## What Are Campaign Use Case Types?

Campaign use case types represent the specific purpose of your SMS or MMS messages. When registering a campaign, you're required to select a use case that accurately describes the nature of your communication - such as customer care, two-factor authentication, or marketing.

Choosing the correct campaign type is essential to meet industry compliance standards, avoid filtering or blocking, and maintain trust with carriers and recipients.

2

## Key Benefits of Choosing the Right Use Case

Selecting the right campaign use case helps ensure that your messages are delivered reliably, compliantly, and with minimal delays in the application process.

**Compliance Assurance** — Reduces the risk of campaign rejection by The Campaign Registry (TCR)

**Carrier Understanding** — Helps carriers understand message intent and enforce spam filtering accurately

**Legal Alignment** — Aligns your messaging practices with industry and legal standards

**Faster Approval** — Supports faster approval and fewer compliance flags during registration

**Improved Deliverability** — Improves long-term deliverability and sender reputation

## Special Campaign Use Cases

Certain campaigns are categorized as "special" by carriers and require additional documentation, verification, or pre-qualification. These often involve sensitive or regulated communications. Special use cases may involve different fees, verification requirements, and carrier-specific restrictions.

Special Use Case

Charity / 501(c)(3) Nonprofit

For registered nonprofit organizations conducting charitable outreach. Must provide IRS nonprofit verification.

Special Use Case

Emergency Services

Used by government agencies to deliver urgent safety or emergency messages. Strictly limited and regulated.

Special Use Case

Political

For political messaging, including campaigns, parties, and PACs. Requires verification through Campaign Verify.

## Low-Volume Use Cases

In addition to the standard "Mixed" or "Marketing" campaign types, there is a dedicated use case called **Low Volume Mixed**. This option is intended for businesses with lighter SMS needs. It supports the same range of message types as a standard Mixed campaign—such as marketing, customer care, and notifications—but comes with a lower monthly fee.

Unlike other campaign types, Low Volume Mixed is locked to the lowest available throughput tier, regardless of the brand's Trust Score. This makes it an ideal, cost-effective choice for small businesses or organizations that don't require high-volume messaging.

## Restricted and Forbidden Use Cases

Not every business category or message type is eligible for A2P 10DLC registration. A Campaign may be rejected, filtered, suspended, or fined if it involves prohibited content, even when the selected Campaign Use Case appears to match the messages being sent.

Before registering a Campaign, confirm that both your business category and planned message content are permitted.

Common restricted or prohibited areas include:

  * High-risk financial services
  * Debt relief, debt collection, or loan-related offers
  * Third-party lead generation
  * Get-rich-quick or work-from-home schemes
  * Illegal substances or controlled products
  * Gambling and sweepstakes
  * Certain sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, and tobacco-related content
  * Messages that violate consent, privacy, or carrier requirements


This list is only a summary. For the complete category definitions, exceptions, enforcement details, and messaging restrictions, see [Forbidden Message Categories for SMS and MMS in the U.S. and Canada.](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001219617>)

Important

Selecting an available Campaign Use Case does not make otherwise prohibited content eligible for messaging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if I choose the wrong campaign use case?

Choosing the wrong use case can lead to campaign rejection, delivery filtering, or compliance issues. Always select the use case that best matches the primary intent of your messages.

Q: Can I register more than one campaign use case?

Yes. If your business sends different types of messages (e.g., marketing and customer support), you may need to register multiple campaigns, each with its appropriate use case.

Q: Can I switch my campaign use case after it's approved?

No. To change your use case, you'll need to register a new campaign with the updated details and sample messages.

Q: What is the difference between Mixed and Low Volume Mixed?

Low Volume Mixed is designed for businesses with lighter messaging needs. It supports the same message types as a standard Mixed campaign but comes with a lower monthly fee and is locked to the lowest throughput tier, regardless of Trust Score.

Q: Why are some use cases marked as "special"?

Special use cases involve sensitive or regulated communications, such as charitable outreach, emergency services, or political messaging. These campaigns require additional documentation and verification before approval.

Q: Can I send messages related to alcohol or tobacco?

Tobacco traffic is prohibited on Toll-Free numbers but allowed on Short or Long Code with proper age-gating procedures. Alcohol traffic is allowed on Toll-Free (U.S. only), Short Code, and Long Code, also with proper age-gating procedures.

Q: What if my business operates in a forbidden use case category?

If your business operates in a forbidden use case category, you will not be able to register a campaign for SMS messaging. Contact HighLevel support if you believe your situation qualifies for an exception.

Q: Where can I find more information about forbidden use cases?

For detailed information on forbidden use cases, consult the carrier compliance documentation or contact HighLevel support for clarification.
