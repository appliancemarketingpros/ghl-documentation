# **What is a Toll-Free Number?**

  


Toll-free numbers are business phone numbers that use recognizable prefixes such as 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, and 833. They can provide businesses with a broader, non-local presence and can support capabilities such as Voice, SMS, and MMS when those capabilities are available for the selected number. Availability depends on the country, number type, selected capabilities, provider inventory, and applicable regulations.

  


A toll-free number purchased through an LC Phone-managed HighLevel sub-account is associated with that specific sub-account. After purchase, the number can be configured for calling, routing, workflows, and other supported communication features.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Toll-Free Numbers**

  


  * **Professional business presence:** Use a recognizable business number that is not tied to a particular local area code.  
  

  * **Voice and messaging capabilities:** Search for toll-free numbers that support capabilities such as Voice, SMS, or MMS when available.  
  

  * **Flexible number selection:** Filter available numbers using number type, capabilities, or specific digits to narrow your search.  
  

  * **HighLevel integration:** Use purchased numbers with supported HighLevel communication, routing, automation, and workflow features.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: What is the benefit of Toll-Free Numbers over Local/Phone numbers?**

Though Toll-Free numbers are high priced they have added advantages to it, please see below:

  * Increased throughput of 3 Message segment per second when compared to 1 message segment per second in case of other 10DLC numbers.the 
  * [US/Canada Only] The Toll-Free Registration helps to send at least a few messages while the registration is in progress unlike the A2P registration for 10DLC numbers.


  


  


**Q: Does purchasing a toll-free number automatically verify it for SMS or MMS?**

No. Purchasing the number and completing Toll-Free Verification are separate processes. Toll-free numbers used for messaging to US or Canadian recipients must successfully complete Toll-Free Verification before SMS or MMS traffic can be sent.

  


**Q: How can one register their US/Canada Toll-Free numbers?**

If the sub-account is on the LC phone, you can sign up for [Toll-Free Number Registration for LC - Phone (US/Canada)](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001222300>) from the application. [Only applicable to US/Canada Toll-Free Numbers]  
  
If the sub-account is using your/their own Twilio account, you will need to contact [Twilio support](<https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us>) to register your toll-free number.If the sub-account is on the LC phone, you can sign up for [Toll-Free Number Registration for LC - Phone (US/Canada)](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001222300>) from the application. [Only applicable to US/Canada Toll-Free Numbers]

  


If the sub-account is using your/their own Twilio account, you will need to contact [Twilio support](<https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us>) to register your toll-free number.

  


**Q: Can I make calls while Toll-Free Verification is pending?**

Yes. Toll-Free Verification applies to messaging. Voice calling is not affected by the Toll-Free Verification status.

  


**Q: How much does a toll-free number cost?**

Phone numbers have recurring rental costs, and rates vary by number type and region. Because phone-system pricing can change, refer to the current [Phone System Pricing & Billing Guide](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001223556>) for current pricing.
