# Generate Submission Documents for Forms, Surveys & Quizzes

Automatically create and store professional PDF documents of every form, survey, and quiz submission in your contact records.

What You'll Learn

This article explains how to enable automatic PDF document generation for form, survey, and quiz submissions in HighLevel. You'll learn how to configure the setting, understand the PDF naming convention, and access generated documents from contact records.

Submission documents provide printable, professional records of responses that are automatically stored and associated with contacts for easy access throughout your CRM workflows.

## What is Generating Submission Documents?

The Generate Submission Document setting allows you to automatically create and store professional PDF copies of every form, survey, or quiz submission. When enabled, HighLevel generates a beautifully formatted document containing all questions and responses, complete with submission metadata.

Each PDF is automatically associated with the submitting contact and stored in their Documents section, making it easy to view, download, share, or use throughout your CRM workflows. This feature is available for Forms, Surveys, and Quizzes across both desktop and mobile experiences.

2

## Key Benefits of Generating Submission Documents

**Automatic PDF Generation** — Every submission creates a printable, professionally formatted PDF document without manual intervention.

**Complete Response Capture** — PDFs include all questions, submitted responses, submission date and time, and contact information.

**Organized Storage** — Documents are automatically stored under Contacts → Documents in dedicated folders (Forms, Surveys, or Quizzes) based on submission type.

**Contact Association** — Each PDF is automatically linked to the submitting contact for easy reference and retrieval.

**CRM Integration** — PDFs can be viewed, downloaded, shared via client portal, printed, and used throughout supported CRM workflows.

**Cross-Platform Support** — Submission documents work seamlessly across desktop and mobile experiences.

## How to Enable Submission Document Generation

Follow these steps to enable automatic PDF generation for form, survey, or quiz submissions:

Step 1

Navigate to Sites

Log into your HighLevel account and click on **Sites** in the main navigation.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/vbw047WbSRdzgJf8AswV+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-29+at+20.21.58.png)

Step 2

Select Form/Surveys/Quiz

Choose whether you want to enable the setting for a Form, Survey, or Quiz. Click the appropriate option from the Sites menu.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077158124/original/iQWypJFqX9-vxgPLf2vf5MkCP7TXf9GnZA.png?1785337232)

Step 3

Edit or Create a Form/Survey/Quiz

Either select an existing form, survey, or quiz from the list, or create a new one by clicking the **\+ Create Form** button (or equivalent for surveys/quizzes). You can start from scratch or use a template.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/lT6yevBQFuYPQy4Wug37+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-29+at+20.31.46.png)

Step 4

Access Settings

Once the form, survey, or quiz builder loads, click on **Settings** in the top navigation or side panel.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/LvfHKY6V4Cbd1nrkAffw+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-29+at+20.33.30.png)

Step 5

Enable Generate Submission Document

Scroll down to the **Form Settings** (or Survey/Quiz Settings) section. Locate the **Generate Submission Document** toggle and switch it to **ON**.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/en9QnvUJ9L6sYF3hJTZN+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-29+at+20.41.08.png)

Step 6

Save Your Changes

Click **Save** to apply the setting. Every new submission from this point forward will automatically generate a PDF document.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/4Ed9IlYlI7Bs5xuh2t0S+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-29+at+20.42.40.png)

Important

The Generate Submission Document setting is configured individually for each form, survey, or quiz. Enabling it on one does not automatically enable it for others. If you want submission documents for multiple forms, you must enable the setting for each one separately.

## PDF Naming Convention

Generated documents follow consistent naming formats to ensure easy identification and organization. The naming convention differs slightly between Forms/Surveys and Quizzes:

Submission Type| PDF Naming Format  
---|---  
Forms & Surveys| [FormName]_[ContactName]_[YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM]  
Quizzes| Result_[FormName]_[ContactName]_[YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM]  
  
Example

**Form/Survey:** ContactForm_JohnDoe_2024-03-15_1430.pdf

**Quiz:** Result_ProductKnowledgeQuiz_JaneSmith_2024-03-15_0920.pdf

This standardized naming convention ensures that PDFs are easily searchable and identifiable within contact records and throughout your CRM.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does enabling the setting generate PDFs for existing submissions?

No. The Generate Submission Document setting only applies to new submissions received after the setting is enabled. Existing submissions from before the setting was turned on will not have PDFs automatically generated.

Q: Can I disable PDF generation after enabling it?

Yes. You can turn the Generate Submission Document toggle OFF at any time. When disabled, new submissions will no longer generate PDF documents. Previously generated PDFs will remain in the contact's Documents section.

Q: Where are the PDFs stored?

Generated PDFs are automatically stored under Contacts → Documents in dedicated folders based on submission type: Forms, Surveys, or Quizzes. Each PDF is associated with the specific contact who completed the submission.

Q: What information is included in the generated PDF?

Each PDF includes all questions from the form, survey, or quiz, the responses submitted by the contact, submission date and time, and contact information. The document is professionally formatted for easy reading and printing.

Q: Do I need to enable this setting for every form, survey, or quiz?

Yes. The Generate Submission Document setting is configured individually for each form, survey, or quiz. Enabling it on one does not automatically enable it for others. This allows you to selectively choose which submissions generate PDFs based on your workflow needs.

Q: Can I share the generated PDFs with contacts?

Yes. Generated PDFs can be downloaded, shared via the client portal, attached to emails, or printed. They integrate with your CRM workflows and can be used anywhere you need a formatted record of the submission.

Q: What happens if a contact submits multiple forms?

Each submission generates a separate PDF document with a unique timestamp in the filename. All PDFs are stored in the contact's Documents section under the appropriate folder (Forms, Surveys, or Quizzes), allowing you to maintain a complete history of all submissions from that contact.
