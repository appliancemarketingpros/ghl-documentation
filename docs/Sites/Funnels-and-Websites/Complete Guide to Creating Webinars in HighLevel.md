## **Webinar Types in HighLevel**  
  


### **Live Webinars**  
  


Live webinars are scheduled events that attendees join at a specific date and time.

Use live webinars for:  
  


  * Product launches  
  

  * Live training  
  

  * Q&A sessions  
  

  * Workshops  
  

  * Coaching sessions


* * *

## **On-Demand Webinars**  
  


On-demand webinars use pre-recorded video content that attendees can watch anytime after registration.

Use on-demand webinars for:  
  


  * Evergreen lead generation  
  

  * Automated sales funnels  
  

  * Training libraries  
  

  * Self-paced education


* * *

  * Live and on-demand webinar support

**Live Webinar**

Required fields:  
  


  * Webinar Name

## **Create an On-Demand Webinar**  
  


Select:  
  
**On-Demand Webinar**

###   
**Required Fields**  
  


On-demand webinars require:  
  


  * Webinar Name  
  

  * Webinar Video or Link  
  


Date, time, and timezone are not required for on-demand webinars.

###   
**Upload Webinar Video**

  
Upload a pre-recorded webinar video or select media from your library.

  
Supported formats include:

  * MP4  
  

  * MOV  
  

  * AVI


###   
**Webinar Cloning**  
  


HighLevel allows users to clone existing webinars for faster setup and reuse.

When cloning a webinar, users can now:  
  


  * Edit webinar date  
  

  * Edit webinar time  
  

  * Reuse webinar templates and settings  
  


This helps simplify recurring webinar scheduling.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**  
  


**Q: What fields are required for live webinars?**  
A: Live webinars require a title, date, time, timezone, and webinar link or video.  
  


**Q: What fields are required for on-demand webinars?**  
A: On-demand webinars require a webinar title and video or webinar link.  
  


**Q: Can webinar dates and times be edited during cloning?**  
A: Yes. Webinar date and time can now be updated during the cloning process.  
  


**Q: Do webinar entries save automatically?**  
A: Yes. Webinar entries are auto-saved and appear immediately in the webinar list.  
  


**Q: Can webinar registrations trigger workflows?**  
A: Yes. Webinar forms can trigger workflows for reminders, follow-ups, tagging, and CRM updates.
