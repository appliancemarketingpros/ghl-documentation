# **What is Blog Post Scheduling in HighLevel?**

  


Blog post scheduling lets you prepare content in advance and set a specific date and time for it to be published. Instead of manually publishing the post later, HighLevel queues the post to publish at the scheduled time.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Scheduling Blog Posts**

  


  * **Plan content in advance:** Prepare blog posts ahead of time and schedule them according to your content calendar.  
  

  * **Control publishing times:** Choose the specific date and time when a blog post should be published.  
  

  * **Automate publishing:** Schedule completed posts to publish later instead of returning to publish them manually.


* * *

## **How to Schedule a Blog Post**

  


Once your blog post content and publishing details are ready, you can choose a future date and time for the post to be published automatically.

  


  1. In your account, go to **Sites > Blogs**.  
  

  2. **Open** the **blog** **site****** where you want to create and schedule the post.  
  


If you do not have a blog yet, click **\+ Create Blog** to create one. (Refer to [Getting Started with Blogs and Blog Posts](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001220286>) for detailed instructions.)  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/xfeA7P1XpAUUFqRZ4xbu+/GIF+Recording+2026-09-03+at+18.32.23.gif)  
  

  3. Click **\+ New Post**.  
  

  4. **Create** and **format** your **blog** **post** **content**. (If you need help creating a blog post, refer to **[Create Blog Posts](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000002450>))**  
  


  5. When your blog post is ready, click **Continue** in the upper-right corner.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/cwnakhdtAFVKaAzTXgYw+/GIF+Recording+2026-09-03+at+20.12.44.gif)  
  

  6. **Complete** the applicable **publishing** **details** , including:  
  


     * URL slug
     * Category
     * Author
     * Canonical link, if applicable
     * Keywords
     * SEO metadata (For detailed instructions on configuring SEO metadata, refer to **[How to Add and Edit SEO Information for Blog Posts](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001220287>)**)  
  

  7. Once the publishing details are complete, select **Schedule**.  
  

  8. Select the **date** when you want the blog post to be published.  
  

  9. Select the **time** by choosing the hour, minute, and AM or PM.  
  

  10. Click **Schedule** in the upper-right corner to confirm.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/qbpiPRxWsdqhbiGJVGoB+/Screen+Shot+2026-09-03+at+20.23.39.png)  
  

  11. The blog post is now scheduled to publish at the selected date and time. You can view the post in your blog post list to confirm that it is scheduled.  
  
![](https://jumpshare.com/share/iHrHHxtqHCa2oo3quj9Q+/Screen+Shot+2026-09-03+at+20.27.41.png)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Do I need to publish a scheduled blog post manually?**  
No. Once the post is successfully scheduled, it is queued to publish at the selected date and time.

  


**Q: How can I tell if my blog post was successfully scheduled?**  
Return to the blog post list after scheduling the post. The post will display its scheduled status.

  


**Q: Can I prepare a blog post before I am ready to schedule it?**  
Yes. You can save a blog post as a draft and complete or schedule it later.

  


**Q: What information should I complete before scheduling a blog post?**  
Complete the applicable publishing information for the post, such as its URL slug, category, author, keywords, and SEO metadata before scheduling it.
