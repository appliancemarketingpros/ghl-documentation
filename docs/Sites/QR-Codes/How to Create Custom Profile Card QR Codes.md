# **What Is a Profile Card QR Code?**

  


A Profile Card QR code opens a mobile-friendly digital profile when scanned. It gives individuals one convenient place to share professional details, contact information, social profiles, booking links, portfolios, and other important resources.

  


Visitors can use the card to call, email, visit a website, open shared links, or save the profile owner’s contact information directly to their device. The card can also include an Exchange Contact form, allowing visitors to submit their own details and become contacts in the CRM. This makes Profile Cards useful for networking, events, sales conversations, email signatures, printed materials, and other situations where someone needs a fast way to connect.

## **Key Benefits of Profile Card QR Codes**

  


  * **Professional digital presence:** Present your professional information through a modern, mobile-friendly page.  
  

  * **Faster contact sharing:** Let visitors save your contact details directly to their device.  
  

  * **Built-in lead capture:** Collect visitor information through an Exchange Contact form and create contacts in the CRM.  
  

  * **Flexible branding:** Customize images, colors, text, icons, and buttons to match your personal or company brand.  
  

  * **Centralized links:** Share websites, booking calendars, portfolios, payment pages, social profiles, and landing pages.  
  

  * **Customizable QR design:** Match the QR code’s colors, frame, logo, background, and supporting text to your campaign.  
  

  * **Flexible sharing:** Download the QR code or copy the direct link for use in digital and printed materials.

## **Built-In and Custom Links**

  


Links turn the Profile Card into a central destination for professional actions such as booking an appointment, viewing a portfolio, making a payment, or connecting on social media. Add only the most useful destinations and arrange them in the order visitors should see them.

  


Built-in social link options include X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and Pinterest.

  


Custom links can be used for destinations such as Booking calendars, Portfolios, Landing Pages or Additional social profiles.

  


Use the built-in link dropdown to add supported social platforms. Select **Add custom links** to enter another destination, name the link, and choose a matching icon.

## **Save Contact**

  


The Save Contact button gives visitors a faster way to add your professional information to their device. It appears on Profile Cards by default and does not require Exchange Contact to be enabled.

  


When a visitor selects **Save Contact** , their device begins the process of adding your contact information. The confirmation or save experience may vary by browser, operating system, and device settings.

  


### **Automatically Download My Contact Info**

  


Starting the contact-download experience automatically makes it easier for visitors to save your information without locating the Save Contact button.

  


Enable **Automatically Download My Contact Info** to initiate the contact-saving experience when the Profile Card opens. The visitor’s browser or device may still require confirmation before downloading or saving the information.

## **Exchange Contact**

  


Exchange Contact allows visitors to submit their own information from your Profile Card. This turns the card into a lead-capture tool while still allowing visitors to view and save your details. When Exchange Contact is enabled, an **Exchange Contact** button appears on the card. Selecting the button opens a form where the visitor can enter their information.

  


When the visitor submits the form, a contact is created in the CRM. Contact activity identifies the Profile Card as the origin of the lead.

  


### **Open Exchange Contact Form by Default**

  


Opening the form automatically places lead capture at the beginning of the visitor experience. This is useful when collecting visitor information is the primary purpose of the Profile Card.

  


Enable **Open Exchange Contact Form by Default** to display the form as soon as the Profile Card opens. Visitors do not need to select the Exchange Contact button first.

## **How to Set Up a Profile Card QR Code**

  


####  _**Step 1:** Open the QR Code Builder_

  


  1. Click **Sites** in the left navigation menu.  
  

  2. Click **QR Codes** in the top navigation bar.  
  

  3. Click **Create QR Code** in the upper-right corner.


  


**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077736980/original/2JOmqUOHsYphSIBdgyXxM1FvyqzFp8RccQ.png?1785958390)**  
  


#### _**Step 2:** Configure QR Code Details_

  


  1. Enter a recognizable name in the **QR name** field.  
  

  2. Select **Profile card** from the available QR types.  
  

  3. Click **Next**.


  


**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737022/original/1dEzbzk3RyxX3fGDBIrOXsTRgY3gCPFEEg.png?1785958502)**  
  


#### _**Step 3:** Enter Your Profile Information_

  


  1. Open the **Info** tab and click **Select** to add a profile picture then select the opacity level (%).  
  

  2. Enter your full name, company name, designation, email address, website, phone number, and address as needed.


  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737149/original/jVBHDo8jQI2DTcaat-uOK8fTT7CQfhVt-Q.png?1785958707)  
  


#### _**Step 4:** Customize the Profile Card Style_

  


  1. Open the **Styles** tab and select a cover-image template, or click **Browse** to add a custom image.  
  

  2. Adjust the image opacity and choose the page, text, icon, button, and button-text colors.  
  

  3. Turn **Show icon label** on or off, then review the card in the live preview.


  


**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737166/original/gEIbPOrJWrWIuE1SG8OMUn1ex94lBlB0rw.png?1785958782)**  
  


#### _**Step 5:** Add and Organize Links_

  


  1. Open the **Links** tab and select the built-in social platforms you want to display, then enter the corresponding URLs or usernames.  
  

  2. Click **Add custom links** to add another destination, enter its name and URL, and choose a matching icon.  
  

  3. Use the drag handles to reorder links or the trash icon to remove them, then review the links in the live preview.


  


**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737232/original/uPulLj9FCOi9YTEuWCXnsL3r8F_dCVNE5A.png?1785958933)**  
  


#### _**Step 6:** Configure Exchange Contact_

  


Exchange Contact turns your Profile Card into a lead-capture tool by letting visitors submit their own contact information. When they complete the form, a new contact is created in the CRM and contact activity identifies the Profile Card as the lead origin.

  


  1. Open the **Exchange contact** tab and turn on **Exchange contact**.  
  

  2. Choose **New form** to create a form for the card, or **Use an existing form** to select a HighLevel form you already created.  
  

  3. Click **Edit form** to update the form without leaving the QR Code Builder, then customize the button text as needed.  
  

  4. Review the Exchange Contact button and form in the live preview.


  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737372/original/2x2uoVdXteTDm5f4d7yOGPG5LP43TUUCew.png?1785959026)

  


  


#### _**Step 7:** Configure Contact Options_

  


  1. Turn on **Open Exchange Contact Form by Default** when the form should appear as soon as the Profile Card opens.  
  

  2. Turn on **Automatically Download My Contact Info** when the contact-saving experience should begin automatically.  
  

  3. Test both settings on a mobile device before sharing the card. Then click **Next.**


  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737409/original/50dPWTLlW4Jls6Nb2H0Rur72rUm2obGUGQ.png?1785959087)

  


  


#### _**Step 8:** Customize the QR Code Design_

  


Customize the QR code so it matches your brand while remaining easy to scan. The design tools control the appearance of the scannable code, not the Profile Card page.

  


  1. Customize the QR code’s colors, dots, markers, border, and frame.  
  

  2. Add a built-in or custom center logo, adjust the logo background, and choose a page background image with the preferred transparency.  
  

  3. Add supporting text, such as “Scan Me” or your name, then choose the font and text color.  
  

  4. Once all customizations have been made, click **Save.**


  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077737460/original/0_CEnIsbNduLCsTGsNHJL7bdxvQ5-agYLQ.png?1785959200)  


  


#### _Step 9: Download or Share the Profile Card_

  


  1. Open the saved QR code from **Sites > QR Codes**.  
  

  2. Download the QR code in PNG, SVG, or PDF format, or copy the Profile Card’s direct link.  
  

  3. Add the QR code or link to email signatures, printed materials, websites, presentations, text messages, or digital campaigns.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Is Exchange Contact required?**

No. Leave Exchange Contact turned off when visitors only need to view and save your contact information.

  


**Q: What is the difference between Save Contact and Exchange Contact?**

Save Contact gives the visitor your information. Exchange Contact asks the visitor to submit their information to you through a form.

  


**Q: Can I use an existing HighLevel form?**

Yes. Enable Exchange Contact and choose **Use an existing form** to select an available form.

  


**Q: Can I change the Exchange Contact button text?**

Yes. You can rename the call-to-action to better match your branding or intended action.

  


**Q: Can I add a booking calendar, portfolio, or payment page?**

Yes. Add the destination as a custom link, enter its URL, and choose a matching icon.
