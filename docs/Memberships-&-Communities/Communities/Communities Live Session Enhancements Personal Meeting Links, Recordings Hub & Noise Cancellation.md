# **What are Communities Live Session Enhancements?**

  


Communities Live Session Enhancements introduce new tools designed to simplify how you host, manage, and reuse live content inside HighLevel Communities. With a reusable Personal Meeting Link, centralized access to recordings, and improved audio clarity, hosts can create a more polished and consistent experience for their audience while reducing manual setup and meeting management tasks.

  


These enhancements are especially useful for recurring coaching calls, onboarding sessions, workshops, office hours, and community-led discussions where consistency and ease of access are important.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Communities Live Session Enhancements**

  


These updates improve both the hosting experience and attendee experience by reducing friction before, during, and after live sessions.  
  


  * **Personal Meeting Links:** Create one reusable meeting room link that can be shared across recurring meetings, events, and 1:1 sessions.  
  

  * **Professional Branding:** Deliver a more consistent and polished experience with a dedicated meeting URL.  
  

  * **Centralized Recordings Hub:** Access, manage, replay, download, or delete recordings from one location.  
  

  * **Content Repurposing:** Turn live sessions into reusable training, onboarding, or educational content.  
  

  * **Enhanced Noise Cancellation:** Reduce background distractions and improve voice clarity during sessions.  
  

  * **Faster Workflows:** Spend less time creating new links and searching for recordings.  
  

  * **Improved Attendee Experience:** Help participants join sessions quickly and enjoy clearer conversations.


* * *

## **Personal Meeting Links**

  


Personal Meeting Links provide hosts with a dedicated, reusable meeting room that stays consistent across sessions. Instead of generating a new link every time you host a session, you can share one permanent link for recurring meetings, office hours, coaching calls, workshops, or community discussions.

* * *

## **Recordings Hub**

  


The Recordings Hub centralizes all session recordings into one accessible location. Hosts can quickly review past sessions, replay important discussions, download recordings for external use, or remove recordings when they are no longer needed.

* * *

## **Enhanced Noise Cancellation**

  


Enhanced Noise Cancellation improves audio quality during live sessions by reducing unwanted background sounds and interruptions. This helps hosts and attendees communicate more clearly, even in less controlled environments.

  


Clearer audio creates a more professional experience and helps maintain participant engagement during meetings and events.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: Can I use the same Personal Meeting Link for recurring sessions?**

Yes. Personal Meeting Links are designed to be reusable across recurring meetings, events, coaching calls, and live sessions.

  


**Q: Where can I find my session recordings?**

All available recordings can be accessed inside the Recordings Hub.

  


**Q: Can I download recordings from the Recordings Hub?**

Yes. Hosts can download recordings for external storage, sharing, or repurposing.

  


**Q: Can recordings be deleted?**

Yes. You can remove recordings directly from the Recordings Hub when they are no longer needed.

  


**Q: Does Enhanced Noise Cancellation work automatically?**

Noise cancellation enhancements are designed to improve audio clarity during live sessions and may be enabled automatically depending on the meeting environment and configuration.

  


**Q: Can attendees join using the same Personal Meeting Link every time?**

Yes. The same reusable meeting link can be shared with attendees for ongoing sessions.

  


**Q: Are Personal Meeting Links useful for 1:1 meetings?**

Yes. Personal Meeting Links work well for recurring client meetings, office hours, consultations, and coaching sessions.

  


**Q: Can recordings be used for onboarding or training?**

Yes. Many hosts use session recordings to create educational libraries, onboarding resources, and member training content.
