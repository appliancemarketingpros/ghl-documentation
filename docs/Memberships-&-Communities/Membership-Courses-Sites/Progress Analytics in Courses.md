## What is Course Progress Analytics?

Course Progress Analytics is a comprehensive dashboard that provides creators and administrators with a centralized view of learner engagement across all courses in their location. The dashboard consolidates key performance metrics, learner journey data, and member-level progress information into a single interface.

Unlike one-off reports, Course Progress Analytics uses consistent filters across all dashboard components, ensuring that every metric updates instantly when you change your view. Whether you're managing a handful of learners or thousands, the dashboard is designed to deliver reliable data for day-to-day decisions.

The analytics dashboard includes filtering options by course and enrollment date, key performance metrics with period-over-period comparisons, a learner journey funnel, a detailed member progress table, and CSV export capabilities.

2

## Key Benefits of Course Progress Analytics

**Unified engagement view** — Understand engagement across all courses from one dashboard, eliminating the need to navigate between multiple reports or export separate data sets.

**Proactive learner support** — Quickly identify learners who need attention before they disengage by reviewing last accessed dates, session counts, and progress percentages.

**Actionable performance metrics** — Measure course performance with clear insights including completion rates, average progress, and time to complete with period-over-period comparisons.

**Flexible reporting** — Export reports to share with teammates or keep for record-keeping. Smaller exports download instantly, while larger exports are delivered by email with a secure 24-hour download link.

**Built for scale** — The dashboard is designed to stay fast as locations and course catalogs grow, with approximately 43% faster page loads and nearly 90% less data downloaded compared to previous implementations.

## Dashboard Components

The Course Progress Analytics dashboard includes five primary components that work together to provide comprehensive learner engagement insights. Each component updates instantly when filters are applied, ensuring consistent data across the entire dashboard.

Component 1

Filters

Apply filters by course and enrollment date to focus on specific segments. The entire dashboard updates instantly using the same filters, ensuring all metrics, charts, and tables reflect your selected criteria.

  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/SXgZ2n8egA2H0wExlpvo+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-30+at+19.41.30.png)

Component 2

Key Performance Metrics

View three essential metrics at a glance, each with a comparison to the previous equivalent time period:

  * **Overall Completion Rate** — The percentage of enrolled learners who have completed the course.
  * **Average Course Progress** — The mean progress percentage across all enrolled learners.
  * **Average Time to Complete** — The average duration from enrollment to course completion.


  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/PGqwckNR2i3WLUvWUVCb+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-30+at+19.43.08.png)

Component 3

User Conversion Funnel

Visualize how learners progress through your course with three funnel stages:

  * **New Users** — Total number of learners who have enrolled in the course.
  * **Started Learning** — Number of enrolled learners who have begun course content.
  * **Completed a Course** — Number of learners who have finished all course requirements.


The funnel helps identify drop-off points and understand where learners may need additional support or where course content may require optimization.

![](https://jumpshare.com/share/XzaQ9Ovc4VGKgKJqnaLS+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-30+at+19.47.40.png)

Component 4

Member Progress Table

View detailed progress information for each learner in a sortable, filterable table. The table includes:

  * **Member name** — The learner's full name.
  * **Courses completed** — Total number of courses the learner has finished.
  * **Enrollment date** — When the learner enrolled in the course.
  * **Course start date** — When the learner first accessed course content.
  * **Last accessed date** — The most recent date the learner interacted with the course.
  * **Session count** — Total number of sessions the learner has logged.


  


![](https://jumpshare.com/share/rFzIVKO6XlPiPgPNJRNq+/Screen+Shot+2026-07-30+at+19.54.17.png)

Component 5

CSV Export

Export exactly what you're viewing on the dashboard to CSV format. Smaller exports download instantly to your browser. Larger exports with 500 or more rows are processed and delivered by email with a secure download link that remains available for 24 hours.

## How to Access Course Progress Analytics

Course Progress Analytics is accessible directly from your HighLevel account. Follow these steps to access the dashboard and begin analyzing learner engagement.

Step 1

Navigate to Course Progress Analytics

From your HighLevel dashboard, navigate to **Memberships → Courses → Analytics → Course progress**.

Step 2

Apply Filters and Analyze Data

Use the course and enrollment date filters to focus on specific segments. All dashboard components update instantly to reflect your selected criteria. Review key metrics, examine the learner journey funnel, and drill into member-level progress in the table.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What metrics are included in the dashboard?

The dashboard includes Overall Completion Rate, Average Course Progress, and Average Time to Complete. Each metric includes a comparison with the previous equivalent time period. The dashboard also displays a learner journey funnel showing enrolled, started learning, and completed counts, plus a detailed member progress table.

Q: How do filters work in Course Progress Analytics?

Apply filters by course and enrollment date at the top of the dashboard. All components — including key metrics, the learner journey funnel, and the member progress table — update instantly using the same filters, ensuring consistent data across the entire dashboard.

Q: How does CSV export work for large data sets?

Smaller exports download instantly to your browser. Larger exports with 500 or more rows are processed on the server and delivered by email with a secure download link. The download link remains valid for 24 hours after the email is sent.

Q: Can I view progress for individual learners?

Yes. The Member Progress Table displays detailed information for each learner including courses completed, enrollment date, course start date, last accessed date, and session count. The table is sortable and searchable for easy analysis.

Q: What does the "Average Time to Complete" metric measure?

Average Time to Complete measures the mean duration from enrollment to course completion across all learners who have finished the course. This metric helps you understand typical completion timelines and identify courses that may require more or less time investment.

Q: How does the period-over-period comparison work?

Each key performance metric includes a comparison with the previous equivalent time period. For example, if you filter data for the last 30 days, the comparison shows the change from the previous 30-day period. This helps you identify trends and measure improvement over time.

Q: Will Course Progress Analytics work with my existing courses?

Yes. Course Progress Analytics works with all existing courses in your location. The dashboard immediately begins tracking engagement data for all current and future courses without requiring any additional configuration.
