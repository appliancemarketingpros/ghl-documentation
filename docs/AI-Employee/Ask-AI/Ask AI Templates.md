## What are Ask AI Templates?

Ask AI Templates are ready-made, guided workflows that walk you through common HighLevel business tasks step by step. Instead of figuring out every click yourself, you select a template and Ask AI collects the information it needs, presents clear choices, and completes the workflow on your behalf.

With over 90 templates covering everyday operations—from creating campaigns and booking appointments to purchasing phone numbers and submitting compliance documents—you can finish complex multi-step tasks in a single guided flow.

2

## Key Benefits

Ask AI Templates streamline your workflow, reduce training time, and keep your team focused on results instead of navigating menus.

**Complete Multi-Step Tasks Faster** — Finish intake forms, booking flows, campaign setup, and billing processes in one guided conversation instead of switching between multiple screens.

**Reduce Onboarding Time** — New users follow templates instead of memorizing HighLevel navigation, so they can start delivering results on day one.

**Organized by Category** — Templates are grouped into clear categories (Communication, CRM, Commerce & Payments, AI Agents, Integrations, Social Media, Sales, Marketing) and sub-areas (Contacts, Calendars, Invoices) so you can find the right workflow quickly.

**Context-Aware Scoping** — Sub-account templates appear when you're working inside a location; agency templates appear when you're in the agency view, keeping your template list focused and relevant.

**Permission-Aware Visibility** — You only see templates your user role is allowed to run. If you lack access to Calendars, Invoices, or other features, those templates stay hidden—keeping the interface clean and secure.

## Sub-Account vs Agency Context

Ask AI Templates automatically adjust to your current context—whether you're working inside a sub-account (location) or viewing agency-level settings.

Sub-Account (Location) Templates

Day-to-Day Location Work

When you're inside a specific sub-account, Ask AI shows templates for workflows that operate at the location level—managing contacts, creating campaigns, booking appointments, sending invoices, and connecting messaging channels. These templates help you complete tasks for individual clients or business units.

Agency (Company) Templates

Agency-Level Operations

When you're in the agency view, Ask AI displays templates for agency-wide tasks—creating users, provisioning new sub-accounts, managing SaaS billing, sharing snapshots across locations, and purchasing phone numbers. These templates appear only in the agency context, keeping location-level menus focused on location work.

Tip

Switch between sub-account and agency views to access the full range of templates. Ask AI automatically updates the template list to match your current working context.

## Permission-Aware Visibility

Ask AI Templates respect your user role permissions. You only see templates you're allowed to run—if your role lacks access to a feature (Contacts, Calendars, Invoices, etc.), the related templates stay hidden.

This permission-aware filtering keeps your template list clean, secure, and relevant. Admins and users with broader permissions will see more templates; restricted users see only the workflows they can execute.

Important

Templates are secured by your user permissions (scopes). If you don't have access to a feature—like Contacts, Calendars, or Invoices—you won't see the related templates. Only users with the required permissions can view and run them.

Example

If a user role has read-only Calendars access (or no Calendars access at all), that user will not see calendar booking templates. If the role gains write access, the calendar templates will appear automatically.

## How to Use Ask AI Templates

Ask AI Templates are designed for zero friction. Follow these steps to launch a guided workflow.

Step 1

Open Ask AI

Click the Ask AI icon in your HighLevel account. The Templates view appears by default.

Step 2

Browse or Search for a Template

Use the category tabs (All, Communication, CRM, Commerce & Payments, AI Agents, Integrations, Social Media, Sales, Marketing) to browse workflows, or type a keyword in the search box to filter by name or description.

Step 3

Select a Template

Click the template card that matches your task. Ask AI will open a guided conversation to collect the information needed for that workflow.

Step 4

Follow the Prompts

Answer Ask AI's questions and make selections as prompted. The assistant will guide you through every required field, choice, and configuration step for the task.

Step 5

Confirm and Complete

Review the summary Ask AI provides, then confirm. The assistant executes the workflow—creating the resource, connecting the integration, or launching the campaign—and confirms completion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why don't I see certain templates?

Templates are filtered by your user permissions. If your role lacks access to a feature—like Contacts, Calendars, or Invoices—the related templates will be hidden. Contact your account admin if you need additional permissions.

Q: Can I customize or edit a template workflow?

Templates are pre-designed workflows that guide you through a specific task. You cannot edit the template itself, but you provide all the custom data (contact names, calendar settings, message text, etc.) during the guided conversation. The assistant applies your inputs to create the final result exactly as you specify.

Q: Do templates work at both the sub-account and agency level?

Yes. Sub-account (location) templates appear when you're inside a specific location and handle day-to-day tasks like campaigns and bookings. Agency (company) templates appear when you're in the agency view and cover agency-wide operations like user provisioning, SaaS billing, and snapshot sharing. The template list adjusts automatically based on your current context.

Q: How many templates are available?

Ask AI includes over 90 templates at launch, covering Communication, CRM, Commerce & Payments, AI Agents, Integrations, Social Media, Sales, and Marketing. HighLevel continues to add templates as new features and workflows are released.

Q: Can I use templates to train new team members?

Absolutely. Templates are ideal for onboarding because they walk new users through each step of a workflow without requiring them to memorize HighLevel navigation. New team members can start completing tasks—launching campaigns, booking appointments, creating pipelines—on day one by following the guided prompts.

Q: What happens if I start a template and need to stop mid-workflow?

You can exit a template conversation at any time. Any changes you confirmed before exiting will be saved (for example, if you completed step 1 of a multi-step template, that step's result is saved). You can start a new template or return to the main Ask AI interface whenever you're ready.

Q: Are templates available on all HighLevel plans?

Ask AI Templates are included with accounts that have access to Ask AI. Check your plan details or contact HighLevel support to confirm availability for your account type.

Q: Can I suggest a new template for a workflow that isn't covered?

Yes. HighLevel welcomes feedback on template ideas. Contact HighLevel support or submit a feature request through the Ideas Board to suggest workflows you'd like to see added to the template library.
