## What is Build with AI?

Build with AI is an intelligent assistant within HighLevel's Flow-Based Builder that allows you to create and modify Voice AI conversation flows using natural language. Instead of manually dragging nodes and configuring actions, you simply describe the Voice AI experience you want to create through chat, and Build with AI generates the complete flow graph for you.

Whether you're starting from scratch or updating an existing flow, Build with AI helps you go from idea to implementation in minutes. The AI understands Voice AI-specific requirements, asks clarifying questions when needed, and creates flows optimized for voice interactions.

Build with AI is purpose-built for Voice AI experiences and understands the capabilities available in Flow-Based Builder, including native actions, Knowledge Base integrations, Web Search, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and other supported tools.

## How Build with AI Works

Build with AI follows a collaborative process to ensure you get exactly the Voice AI flow you need. The workflow includes understanding your requirements, creating a build plan, generating the flow, and allowing you to review before applying.

Step 1

Describe Your Requirements

You chat with Build with AI and describe the Voice AI experience you want to create. Explain what the Voice AI should do, what information it should collect, and how it should respond to different scenarios.

Step 2

AI Asks Follow-Up Questions

If needed, Build with AI asks clarifying questions to better understand your workflow requirements. This ensures the generated flow matches your specific business process and conversation logic.

Step 3

Review the Build Plan

Build with AI creates a build plan outlining the flow structure, actions, and conversation logic it will implement. You can review this plan and request changes before the flow is generated.

Step 4

Preview the Generated Graph

Build with AI generates the complete flow graph based on your requirements and the approved build plan. You can preview the generated graph and see all nodes, connections, and configurations.

Step 5

Apply to Canvas

Once you're satisfied with the generated flow, apply it to your canvas. Build with AI adds the flow to your Flow-Based Builder workspace where you can further customize it or immediately start using it.

## How to Use Build with AI

Build with AI is integrated directly into the Flow-Based Builder interface with a split-screen layout that makes it easy to describe your requirements and see the generated flow in real time.

Understanding the Interface

The Build with AI interface features a two-panel layout:

**Left Panel (Chat)** — The conversational interface where you describe your Voice AI requirements, review build plans with checkmarks showing completed steps, and interact with the AI to refine your flow.

**Right Panel (Canvas)** — The visual flow builder where you see the generated graph with all nodes, connections, and routing logic. You can zoom, pan, and inspect nodes before applying the flow to your workspace.

### Creating a New Flow

When creating a new flow, describe the Voice AI experience you want to build in the chat panel. Be specific about what the Voice AI should do, what information it should collect, and how it should handle different caller scenarios. Build with AI will generate a complete flow graph that appears in the canvas panel for your review.

Pro Tip

The more specific you are in your description, the better Build with AI can match your requirements. Include details about caller types, information to collect, decision points, and what actions to take based on caller responses.

### Editing Existing Flows

To modify an existing flow, describe the changes you want to make in the chat panel. You can add new capabilities, modify conversation logic, update actions, or restructure parts of the flow. Build with AI understands your current flow and applies the requested changes while preserving the rest of your configuration.

### Reviewing and Applying Your Flow

After Build with AI generates your flow:

  * Review the build plan in the chat panel (marked with green checkmarks when approved)
  * Examine the flow graph in the canvas panel to see all nodes and connections
  * Click on individual nodes to inspect their configurations and actions
  * Request changes through the chat if you want to modify the flow
  * Apply the flow to your workspace when you're satisfied with the result


Remember

The flow is not applied to your workspace until you explicitly choose to apply it. You can preview, request changes, and iterate as many times as needed before committing the flow to your canvas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can Build with AI modify existing flows, or only create new ones?

Build with AI can both create new flows from scratch and modify existing flows. When editing an existing flow, describe the changes you want to make, and Build with AI will apply those updates while preserving the rest of your flow configuration.

Q: What actions and tools can Build with AI use in generated flows?

Build with AI understands all capabilities available in Flow-Based Builder, including native actions, Knowledge Base integrations, Web Search, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and other supported tools. It intelligently orchestrates these actions based on your workflow requirements.

Q: Do I have to accept the flow as generated, or can I request changes?

You can request changes at any point in the process. After reviewing the build plan or the generated flow graph, you can ask Build with AI to make modifications. The flow is only applied to your canvas when you explicitly choose to apply it.

Q: How does Build with AI understand Voice AI-specific requirements?

Build with AI is purpose-built for Voice AI experiences and trained to understand the nuances of voice conversations. It generates flows optimized for voice interactions, including appropriate conversation logic, response handling, and caller experience patterns specific to voice communication.

Q: Does Build with AI use templates, or does it create custom flows?

Build with AI does not use fixed templates or predefined architectures. It understands your unique workflow requirements and generates a custom graph that matches your specific conversation logic and business process. Each flow is tailored to your described requirements.

Q: Can I manually edit a flow after Build with AI creates it?

Yes. Once Build with AI applies a flow to your canvas, it becomes a standard Flow-Based Builder flow that you can manually edit, customize, or expand using the visual flow editor. You have full control to make any additional changes after the AI generates the initial flow.

Q: What happens if Build with AI asks follow-up questions?

If Build with AI needs more information to accurately create your flow, it asks clarifying questions in the chat. Answer these questions to help Build with AI better understand your workflow requirements. This ensures the generated flow matches your specific business process and conversation logic.

Q: How do I access Build with AI?

Build with AI is available directly within the Flow-Based Builder interface. Access it from your Flow-Based Builder workspace to start creating or modifying Voice AI flows through the chat interface.
