# Working Hours for Conversation AI

Control when your AI agents respond by setting specific working days, hours, and after-hours behavior for each channel.

What You'll Learn

This article explains how to configure Working Hours for your Conversation AI agents so they respond only during your business operating hours.

You'll learn how to set flexible schedules, choose which channels follow those hours, configure after-hours auto-replies, notify your team, and automatically follow up when business resumes.

Labs Feature

Working Hours is available through HighLevel Labs. Enable the feature flag in Settings → Labs before configuring it for your agents.

## What is Working Hours for Conversation AI?

Working Hours gives you control over when your Conversation AI agents actively respond to customers. Instead of replying 24/7, you can set specific working days and hours for each agent, choose which channels should follow those hours, and configure what happens when customers reach out after business hours.

This feature helps businesses align AI agent availability with their actual operating hours while still acknowledging and following up with customers who message outside those times.

Working Hours is optional. If you do not enable it, your agent continues responding at any time as usual.

2

## Key Benefits

Working Hours provides several advantages for businesses that want more control over AI agent availability:

**Flexible Scheduling** — Set different working days and hours for each agent, including multiple time slots per day for breaks or split shifts.

**Channel-Specific Control** — Choose which channels follow the schedule (SMS, Email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Chat Widget, Live Chat, TikTok) while others continue responding 24/7.

**Timezone Flexibility** — Run the schedule based on each contact's local timezone or your business timezone.

**Ongoing Conversation Support** — Allow the agent to continue replying after hours if a conversation started during working hours, until it becomes inactive.

**After-Hours Acknowledgement** — Automatically send a custom auto-reply to customers who message outside working hours, with support for custom values.

**Team Notifications** — Send email notifications to admins, users, assigned users, selected team members, or custom email addresses when customers message after hours.

**Contact Tagging** — Automatically add tags to contacts who message outside business hours, making it easier to identify and follow up with them.

**Automatic Follow-Up** — Send a follow-up message when business hours resume if the customer has not received a response from the agent or a team member.

3

## How Working Hours Operates

Working Hours controls when your AI agent actively responds based on your configured schedule. Here's how it functions:

During Working Hours

Active Response

The agent responds to customer messages on all configured channels according to your agent's settings.

Outside Working Hours

Inactive on Selected Channels

The agent does not respond on channels included in the Working Hours schedule. Channels not included continue responding normally.

Ongoing Conversations

Continued Support

If a conversation started during working hours, the agent can continue replying after closing time until the conversation becomes inactive.

Per-Agent Configuration

Independent Schedules

Working Hours is configured per agent, so different agents can have completely different schedules.

Suggestive Mode

In Suggestive Mode, suggestions can still be generated after working hours for your team.

Manual Activation

Agents can still be manually activated outside their Working Hours when needed via the conversation panel.

## How to Configure Working Hours

Follow these steps to set up Working Hours for your Conversation AI agent:

Step 1

Enable the Labs Feature Flag

Go to **Settings → Labs** and search for "Working Hours". Enable the feature flag.

Step 2

Navigate to Your Agent Settings

Open **AI Agents → Conversation AI**. Select the agent you want to configure.

Step 3

Access Working Hours Settings

Go to the **Deploy** tab and select **Working Hours**.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155080680531/original/ELLu2sq-MsjWnL11aUJNqv_gtoFswQpzpA.png?1789116587)

Step 4

Enable Active Hours

Toggle **Active Hours** on to activate the Working Hours feature for this agent.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155080680623/original/i4mVeok55HYSbsuKKY53eLmh6Ojy0XRcFg.png?1789116622)

Step 5

Select Channels

Choose which channels should follow the Working Hours schedule. Available channels include SMS, Email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Chat Widget, Live Chat, and TikTok. Channels not selected continue responding 24/7.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155080680713/original/rQ-a1w-xcSv270Wzj366xhcLrs_DQP06Hw.png?1789116653)

Choose Timezone

Select **Contact Timezone** to run the schedule based on each contact's local timezone, or **Business Timezone** to use your business timezone for all contacts.

Set the days and times when the agent should actively respond. You can set different hours for each day and add multiple time slots per day to accommodate breaks or split shifts.

Optionally, configure after-hours behavior including ongoing conversations, auto-replies, team notifications, contact tags, and follow-up messages. See the next section for details.

Save Your Settings

## After-Hours Options

Working Hours includes several optional features to help you manage customer interactions outside business hours:

Feature 1

Continue Ongoing Conversations

Allow the agent to keep replying after closing time if a conversation started during working hours. The agent continues until the conversation becomes inactive.

Feature 2

Off-Hours Auto-Reply

Automatically acknowledge customers who message outside working hours. Add your own custom message and personalize it using supported custom values. The auto-reply is triggered once per closed period.

Feature 3

Team Notifications

Send email notifications when customers message after hours. Choose recipients from admins, users, assigned users, selected team members, or custom email addresses. Notifications are triggered once per closed period.

Feature 4

Contact Tags

Automatically add selected tags to contacts who message outside business hours. This makes it easier to identify, filter, and follow up with after-hours contacts. Tags are applied once per closed period.

Feature 5

Follow Up When Business Hours Resume

Automatically send a follow-up message when business reopens if the customer has not received a response from the agent or a team member. This ensures no after-hours inquiries are left unattended.

One-Time Triggers

The off-hours auto-reply, notifications, and contact tags are triggered once per closed period. Customers do not receive the same acknowledgement for every message they send after hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if I don't enable Working Hours?

If Working Hours is not enabled, your agent continues responding at any time as it does today. Working Hours is optional.

Q: Do Working Hours apply to all channels?

Working Hours apply only to the channels you select during configuration. Other channels continue working normally without any schedule restrictions.

Q: How often are after-hours auto-replies sent?

The off-hours auto-reply is triggered once per closed period, so customers do not receive the same acknowledgement for every message they send after hours.

Q: What happens to follow-up messages if the customer already received a response?

The business-hours follow-up is skipped if the customer has already received a response from either the AI agent or a team member.

Q: Do Working Hours affect Suggestive Mode?

In Suggestive Mode, suggestions can still be generated after working hours for your team.

Q: What happens if I also have Advanced Follow-Up configured with Followup Hours?

If Followup Hours is also configured for Advanced Follow-Up, the agent's Working Hours takes priority for the channels included in the agent's schedule.

Q: Can I manually activate an agent outside its Working Hours?

Yes. Agents can still be manually activated outside their Working Hours when needed via the conversation panel.

Q: Can I set different Working Hours for different agents?

Yes. Working Hours is configured per agent, so different agents can have completely different schedules.
