## What is the AI Suite Walkthrough Course?

The AI Suite Walkthrough Course is a comprehensive training program available in HighLevel University that provides a broad overview of HighLevel's core AI products. This course helps users understand the platform's AI ecosystem and get up and running with six essential AI tools.

As HighLevel's AI offerings have expanded, this course serves as a launchpad for users to gain clarity on the big picture of AI in the platform. It covers each tool's primary use case and provides practical setup and implementation instructions, making it the perfect starting point before diving into more specialized AI courses.

The course complements tool-specific training by giving users one-stop access to understand how all AI products work together within HighLevel's ecosystem.

## How to Access the Course

The AI Suite Walkthrough Course is available in the HighLevel University Community Learning Tab. Follow these steps to access the course:

Step 1

Navigate to HighLevel University

From your Agency Account, click the **University** tab in the navigation menu.

Click the **Go to HighLevel University** button to open the University portal.

Step 2

Access the Learning Tab

Once in HighLevel University, click the **Learning** tab to view all available courses and training materials.

Step 3

Find the AI Suite Walkthrough Course

Locate the **AI Suite Walkthrough** course in the Learning Tab and click to begin your training.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Who should take the AI Suite Walkthrough Course?

This course is ideal for any HighLevel user who wants to understand and implement AI automation. Whether you're an agency owner, marketing professional, or business consultant, this course provides the foundational knowledge needed to leverage HighLevel's AI products effectively.

Q: Do I need prior experience with AI to take this course?

No prior AI experience is required. The course is designed to provide a comprehensive overview starting from the basics, making it accessible for users at all skill levels while still offering valuable insights for experienced users.

Q: How long does the course take to complete?

The course is self-paced, allowing you to complete it according to your schedule. Most users can work through the material in a few hours, but you can take as much time as needed to fully understand each AI product.

Q: Are there additional specialized AI courses available?

Yes, HighLevel University offers more specialized AI courses that dive deeper into specific tools and use cases. The AI Suite Walkthrough Course serves as the perfect foundation before exploring these advanced training programs.

Q: Can I access the course materials after completing it?

Yes, once you access the course through HighLevel University, you can return to the materials at any time for reference and review.

Q: Do I need access to all AI products to benefit from this course?

While having access to the AI products allows you to practice implementation, the course provides valuable knowledge even if you don't currently use all features. The training helps you understand which AI tools would benefit your business and how to prepare for their implementation.

Q: Is this course available to all HighLevel users?

Yes, the AI Suite Walkthrough Course is available to all HighLevel users through the University Community Learning Tab. Simply navigate to HighLevel University from your Agency Account to access the course.

Q: Will the course content be updated as AI products evolve?

HighLevel University regularly updates course content to reflect product enhancements and new features. The AI Suite Walkthrough Course will be maintained to provide current and accurate information about HighLevel's AI capabilities.
