## **What are Contextual In-App Nudges?**

  


Contextual In-App Nudges automatically appear during onboarding when HighLevel detects that additional guidance may help you complete a setup task. Depending on your progress, a nudge may display one or more relevant resources—such as support articles, tutorial videos, implementation calls, or Support AI—to help you continue without interrupting your workflow.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Contextual In-App Nudges**

  


This feature is crafted to enhance the user experience by reducing friction and delivering help at just the right moment. Its benefits include:

  


  * Reducing onboarding friction by offering immediate, context-sensitive support.  
  


  * Driving higher action completions on LaunchPad, which contributes to improved trial-to-paid conversions.  
  


  * Providing personalized assistance through dynamic links that directly address where users are stuck.  
  


  * Enhancing overall user satisfaction by streamlining the onboarding process with minimal interruption.


* * *

## **Dynamic Contextual Link Options**

  


Dynamic Contextual Link Options integrate multiple support resources into one seamless nudge, ensuring that users find the exact help they need without leaving their workflow. This approach optimizes self-service by connecting users directly to relevant assistance.

  


  * **Support Article** – Directly opens detailed documentation for in-depth guidance.  
  


  * **Tutorial Video** – Offers visual, step-by-step instructions for a more intuitive learning process.  
  


  * **Kickoff/Implementation Call Link** – Simplifies scheduling of personalized implementation or strategy sessions.  
**  
**

  * **Chat with Support AI** – Provides real-time interactive support to quickly resolve issues.


* * *

## **Automatic Nudge Suppression**

Automatic Nudge Suppression refines the user experience by eliminating redundant prompts. When any user within a sub-account successfully completes a particular action, all contextual nudges for that action are automatically suppressed for everyone in the sub-account. 

This smart function minimizes distractions and ensures that nudges only appear when truly needed.

## **Example Contextual In-App Nudges**

  


Getting started with Contextual In-App Nudges is straightforward since the feature is automatically rolled out on a going-forward basis for all plans. Follow these steps to observe and benefit from the feature in your HighLevel account:

  


  1. Log in to your HighLevel account and navigate to the LaunchPad section.  
  

  2. Begin an onboarding action—whether that’s starting a guided tour, watching a tutorial, or engaging with a flow.  
  

  3. When you pause, abandon, or linger during an action, a contextual nudge will appear featuring one or more dynamic links.  
  


Click on any available link (Support Article, Tutorial Video, Kickoff/Implementation Call, or Chat with Support AI) to immediately access the assistance you need.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q. When do Contextual In-App Nudges appear?**

Contextual In-App Nudges may appear when a user starts an onboarding or LaunchPad-related action but does not complete it, leaves the flow, or spends additional time on a tutorial or setup task.

  


  


**Q. What can a Contextual In-App Nudge include?**

A nudge may include one or more helpful resources, such as:

  * A relevant support article
  * A tutorial video
  * A kickoff or implementation call link
  * Chat with Support AI


The resources shown can vary depending on the context.

  


  


**Q. Why am I not seeing a Contextual In-App Nudge?**

You may not see a nudge if:

  * The related onboarding action has already been completed.
  * Another user in the same sub-account has completed the action, causing the nudge to be suppressed.
  * LaunchPad is not available for your current plan, sub-account, or user role.
  * The system has not detected a need to show a contextual nudge.


  


  


**Q. Why did a nudge disappear?**

Contextual nudges are automatically suppressed once the related action is completed.

If any user in the same sub-account completes the action, the related nudge may be removed for all users in that sub-account.

  


  


**Q. Will every nudge show the same resources?**

No. Contextual nudges can display different resources depending on the action or task being completed.

A nudge may include one or more relevant links rather than showing every available resource each time.

  


  


**Q. Can I manually trigger a Contextual In-App Nudge?**

Contextual nudges are displayed automatically based on user activity and onboarding progress. They are not manually triggered by the user.

  


  


**Q. Can I dismiss a nudge?**

If the current product behavior allows users to dismiss the nudge, keep the existing documented behavior here. If dismissal is not explicitly documented, I would avoid adding this FAQ until the behavior is confirmed.

  


  


**Q. Why does another user in my sub-account no longer see the nudge?**

Nudge suppression applies at the sub-account level. Once the related action is completed by any user in the sub-account, the associated nudge may be suppressed for everyone in that sub-account.
