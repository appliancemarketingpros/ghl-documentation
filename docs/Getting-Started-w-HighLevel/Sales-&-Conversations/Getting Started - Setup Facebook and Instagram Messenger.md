## **Integrate Facebook & Instagram**

  


First, let's get Facebook and Instagram working together within your CRM. This integration will be a game changer in how quickly and effectively you communicate with customers.

  * Head over to **Settings > Integrations**.  
  

  * Click on **Connect** under Facebook & Instagram.  
  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155046961045/original/QdZFNRgoafMeGOzeflCNcbwiaqz-QRd2Jg.png?1747785916)  
  

  * **Log in** to Facebook and choose your Facebook **page** (make sure it's the one **connected to Instagram**).  
  

  * Click **Connect Facebook & Instagram**.  
  

  * Lastly, hit **Sync Leads**.

## **How Facebook and Instagram messages create contacts**

  


When someone messages your business on Facebook or Instagram for the first time, HighLevel creates or updates a contact based on your **Contact Deduplication Preferences**.

  


  * If the person later shares an email or phone number that matches an existing contact and **Allow Duplicate Contact** is turned off, HighLevel automatically merges the messenger contact into that existing record so you keep one complete profile.  
  

  * If **Allow Duplicate Contact** is turned on, the automatic merge does not run and separate contact records may exist for the same person.

## **Link Instagram to Facebook**

  


Now, let's ensure Instagram and Facebook are fully linked so your messages flow seamlessly into one inbox. Trust us—this step will save you tons of time!  
  


  * Navigate to your **Facebook account** and log in to your page.  
  

  * Click on **Account > Settings**.  
  

  * Use the search bar to find **Linked Accounts**.  
  

  * Select **Instagram** and click **Connect**.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155046961111/original/qiMpvvQcdL5_5wGdinP4dhnA4sykmmaXRw.png?1747786378)  
  

  * Keep the toggle on to **enable Instagram messages** to appear in your inbox.  
  

  * **Authenticate** your Instagram login credentials.  
  


Fantastic job! You're now set up to effortlessly handle your social interactions. Next, consider testing the integration by sending a test message to confirm everything is working smoothly.

## **Test Your Facebook & Instagram Messenger Connection**

  


After connecting your Facebook Page and Instagram account, send a test message to confirm that incoming messages are reaching HighLevel correctly.  
  


### 
    
    
    **Tip:** Send the test message from a different Facebook or Instagram account—not the business account you connected to HighLevel. This provides a more accurate test of the customer messaging experience.

  


### **Test Facebook Messenger**  
  


  1. From a different Facebook account, open the connected business's **Facebook Page**.  
  


  2. Click **Message** and send a test message to the Page.  
  


  3. In HighLevel, open the appropriate **Sub-Account**.  
  


  4. Go to **Conversations**.  
  


  5. Locate the new conversation in the Conversations inbox.  
  


  6. Open the conversation and confirm that the Facebook Messenger test message appears in the message history.  
  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078682474/original/FTxoEww4wAUJl0Rp_Fq2AWylS1ZoqNyTLA.png?1787050147)


###   


### **Test Instagram Messaging**  
  


  1. From a different Instagram account, open the connected business's **Instagram profile**.  
  


  2. Send a new direct message (DM) to the account.  
  


  3. In HighLevel, open the appropriate **Sub-Account**.  
  


  4. Go to **Conversations**.  
  


  5. Locate the new conversation in the Conversations inbox.  
  


  6. Open the conversation and confirm that the Instagram test message appears in the message history.


###   


### **What Does a Successful Connection Look Like?**

  
Your integration is working correctly when:  
  


  * The test message appears in **HighLevel → Conversations**.  
  


  * The message appears under the correct contact/conversation.  
  


  * You can open the conversation and view the incoming Facebook Messenger or Instagram message.


  
![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078706715/original/xGnLhZqZULa9VCCb21fzNJofyUmRGHhYtQ.png?1787060979)  
  


When someone messages your business through Facebook or Instagram for the first time, HighLevel creates or updates their contact based on your **Contact Deduplication Preferences**.

###   


### **Test the Reply**

  


For an end-to-end verification, reply to the test message from within **HighLevel → Conversations**. Then, return to the Facebook Messenger or Instagram account you used for testing and confirm that the reply was received.  
  


If both the incoming message and your reply are delivered successfully, your messaging connection is working as expected.

###   


### **What If the Test Message Doesn't Appear?**

  
If the test message doesn't appear in **Conversations** :  
  


  1. Go to **Settings → Integrations** and confirm that the correct Facebook Page and Instagram account are connected.  
  


  2. Confirm that messaging access is enabled for the connected account.  
  


  3. Verify that the appropriate Facebook/Instagram permissions have been granted.  
  


  4. For Instagram, confirm that the Instagram account is correctly linked to the Facebook Page.  
  


  5. Send another test message after verifying the connection and permissions.


  


If messages still aren't appearing, see [Messaging Setup & Troubleshoot](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006069>) for additional Facebook and Instagram messaging checks.
