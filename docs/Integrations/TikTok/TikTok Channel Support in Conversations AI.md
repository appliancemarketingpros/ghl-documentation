## What is TikTok Channel Support?

TikTok channel support enables Conversations AI to engage with customers directly through TikTok messages. This integration brings AI-driven automation to TikTok conversations, allowing businesses to respond instantly, qualify leads, answer questions, and guide customers toward desired outcomes without manual intervention.

All supported Conversations AI capabilities that work on other channels are available for TikTok, providing consistent automation across your entire messaging ecosystem. The AI bot handles eligible TikTok conversations automatically based on your configuration, ensuring no customer message goes unanswered.

TikTok appears alongside other supported channels like SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Live Chat, Email, and Chat Widget in your bot configuration, giving you centralized control over how Conversations AI operates across all connected platforms.

## How to Enable TikTok in Conversations AI

Follow these steps to enable TikTok channel support for your Conversations AI bot:

Step 1

Navigate to Conversations AI Settings

Go to the **AI Agents** section in your HighLevel account by clicking **AI Agents** in the left navigation menu. Navigate to the **Conversation AI** tab to access your bot configurations.

You will see a list of existing Conversation AI agents or the option to create a new one.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078027529/original/e4i1Wj_jnL80rDGfoVcJqNVIEK7fRt1MBw.png?1786366251)

Step 2

Select or Create Your Bot

From the **Agents List** view, select the existing bot you want to use for TikTok conversations by clicking the **Edit** option from the three-dot menu next to the bot name, or create a new bot if needed.

Ensure your bot has the appropriate knowledge base, personality settings, goals, and conversation behavior configured before proceeding.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078027811/original/-KOJoln6v9Pq7yd3N8KtvI02XJZZz211Nw.png?1786366355)

Step 3

Access Bot Settings and Navigate to Deploy Tab

Once you've opened your bot for editing, you'll see the bot configuration interface with multiple tabs including **Build** and **Deploy**.

Click on the **Deploy** tab to access channel configuration options. This is where you'll enable TikTok as a supported channel for your bot.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078028023/original/58dYUZnrnJcCK8om05N_ECI7ZIQhO3qB_A.png?1786366436)

Step 4

Review Available Communication Channels

In the Deploy section, you'll see channel cards for all available communication platforms including SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Live Chat, Chat Widget, and Email.

Each channel card displays the channel name, icon, a brief description, and a **Configure** button.

Step 5

Locate and Configure the TikTok Channel

Find the **TikTok** channel card, which displays the TikTok icon and the description "Connect your agent to your TikTok account(s) and let it respond to direct messages from your customers."

Click the **Configure** button on the TikTok channel card to open the TikTok channel configuration modal.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155078028058/original/IgINs5rurnDNtDSfs_6SppEBVfwhLVhPZA.png?1786366464)

Step 6

Enable TikTok Channel Assignment

In the **Edit channel** modal that opens, you'll see the TikTok icon at the top and an **Assign channel** toggle switch.

Toggle the **Assign channel** switch to the **on** position (blue/enabled state) to activate TikTok for this bot.

Step 7

Select TikTok Account(s)

After enabling the toggle, you'll see a **Select account(s)** dropdown field marked as required (indicated by a red asterisk).

Click the dropdown and select which TikTok account(s) you want this bot to handle. You can select individual accounts or use the **Select all** checkbox to apply the bot to all connected TikTok accounts.

The dropdown displays all TikTok accounts that are connected and available in your HighLevel instance.

Step 8

Configure Tag-Based Filters (Optional)

Below the account selection, you'll see optional filtering options including **Has tags** and **Doesn't have tags** sections.

These filters allow you to control which TikTok conversations the bot handles based on contact tags. You can configure the bot to only engage with conversations where contacts have specific tags (using the **Has tags** filter) or exclude conversations where contacts have certain tags (using the **Doesn't have tags** filter).

Each filter section includes **AND** / **OR** logic options, allowing you to create sophisticated filtering rules.

If you don't configure tag filters, the bot will handle all eligible TikTok conversations from the selected accounts.

Step 9

Save TikTok Channel Configuration

Once you've selected your TikTok account(s) and configured any desired tag filters, click the **Update** button at the bottom right of the modal to save your TikTok channel configuration.

You can click **Cancel** if you want to discard changes and close the modal without saving.

Step 10

Save Overall Bot Configuration

After closing the TikTok channel configuration modal, click the **Save** button in the top-right corner of the bot configuration interface to apply all changes to your bot, including the newly enabled TikTok channel.

Success

TikTok is now enabled for Conversations AI. The bot will automatically engage with eligible TikTok conversations from the selected accounts based on your configuration. You can view and manage TikTok conversations in your unified Conversations inbox alongside messages from all other channels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a separate bot for TikTok, or can I use the same bot across multiple channels?

You can use the same Conversations AI bot across multiple channels, including TikTok. Simply enable TikTok as a supported channel within your existing bot configuration. The bot will apply the same knowledge base, behavior, and automation rules to TikTok conversations.

Q: What happens if a TikTok conversation requires human assistance?

Conversations AI hands over the conversation to a human team member when needed. The handoff preserves full conversation context, so your team can continue the conversation seamlessly without asking the customer to repeat information.

Q: Where can I view TikTok conversations handled by the bot?

All TikTok conversations appear in your unified Conversations inbox alongside messages from all other channels. You can filter, sort, and manage TikTok conversations from the same interface you use for SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, and other channels.

Q: Can I customize which TikTok conversations are handled by Conversations AI?

Yes, you can configure which conversations are eligible for AI handling using tag-based filters in the channel configuration. Use the "Has tags" and "Doesn't have tags" filter options to control which TikTok conversations the bot engages with based on contact tags.

Q: Will Conversations AI respond to TikTok messages outside of business hours?

Yes, Conversations AI continues engaging customers on TikTok 24/7, even when your team is unavailable. This ensures no customer message goes unanswered regardless of when it arrives.

Q: What information does Conversations AI need to answer TikTok questions accurately?

Conversations AI uses the knowledge base you've configured for the bot. Ensure your bot's knowledge base contains accurate, up-to-date business information so the AI can provide helpful answers to TikTok customer questions.

Q: Can Conversations AI book appointments directly in TikTok messages?

Yes, Conversations AI can guide TikTok conversations toward appointment booking. The bot collects necessary information and facilitates scheduling directly within the TikTok conversation flow.

Q: Can I enable TikTok for multiple accounts at once?

Yes, when configuring the TikTok channel, you can select multiple TikTok accounts or use the "Select all" option to enable the bot for all connected TikTok accounts simultaneously.

Q: Do I need to configure anything else after enabling TikTok in my bot?

Once you enable TikTok as a supported channel, select your TikTok account(s), and save your settings, Conversations AI is ready to handle TikTok conversations. No additional setup is required unless you want to add tag-based filters or customize specific behavior for TikTok.
