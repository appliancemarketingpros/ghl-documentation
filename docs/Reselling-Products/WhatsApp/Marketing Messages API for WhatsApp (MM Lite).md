## **What is MM Lite?**

  


Marketing Messages Lite, or MM Lite, is Meta’s optimized delivery system for eligible WhatsApp marketing messages in HighLevel. It helps businesses send promotional WhatsApp messages more efficiently by prioritizing delivery to contacts who are more likely to engage. MM Lite is designed for marketing use cases, while standard WhatsApp messaging continues to support conversations, support, and other non-marketing communication types.

  


MM Lite helps businesses improve WhatsApp marketing performance without rebuilding existing templates, workflows, or campaigns. Once enabled, eligible marketing messages can use optimized delivery logic while your existing WhatsApp setup continues to work as expected.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of MM Lite**

  


MM Lite is useful for businesses that send WhatsApp marketing messages at scale and want better control over delivery timing, audience engagement, and campaign efficiency. These benefits help protect the customer experience while supporting stronger marketing outcomes.

  


  * **Optimized Delivery:** MM Lite helps prioritize eligible WhatsApp marketing messages for contacts who are more likely to engage.  
  

  * **Improved Marketing Efficiency:** Marketing campaigns can be sent with smarter delivery logic instead of relying only on broad message distribution.  
  

  * **Time-Based Messaging:** Time-to-Live, or TTL, helps prevent outdated marketing messages from being delivered after they are no longer relevant.  
  

  * **No Template Rebuild Required:** Existing approved WhatsApp templates can continue to work after MM Lite is enabled.  
  

  * **Workflow Continuity:** Existing workflows, automations, and campaigns continue running without disruption after activation.  
  

  * **Marketing-Focused Sending:** MM Lite is designed for marketing messages, while standard WhatsApp messaging remains available for support, conversations, and other applicable message types.


* * *

## **When To Use MM Lite**

  


MM Lite is best suited for promotional WhatsApp messaging where timing, engagement, and audience quality matter. Understanding when MM Lite applies helps you choose the right sending approach for each type of customer communication.

  


Use MM Lite for eligible WhatsApp marketing messages, such as:

  


  * Promotions
  * Offers
  * Re-engagement campaigns
  * Campaign broadcasts
  * Marketing follow-ups
  * Product or service announcements


  


Continue using standard WhatsApp messaging for non-marketing use cases, such as:

  


  * Customer support conversations
  * Service conversations
  * Transactional or utility updates
  * Authentication messages
  * Free-form replies within an active customer service window


  


WhatsApp messages are categorized into Marketing, Utility, Authentication, and Service message types, and each category can follow different pricing and sending behavior. For pricing and category details, review the [WhatsApp Pricing, Billing, and Rebilling Guide. ](<https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001428-whatsapp-pricing-billing-and-rebilling-guide>)

## **Prerequisites To Enable MM Lite**

  


MM Lite requires the correct WhatsApp and Meta account access before activation. Confirming these prerequisites helps avoid setup delays and ensures the upgrade can be approved from the correct business account.

  


Before enabling MM Lite, make sure you have:

  


  * An active WhatsApp setup in HighLevel.  
  

  * Access to the connected Facebook Business Manager.  
  

  * A WhatsApp Business Account linked to the correct business portfolio.  
  

  * Permission to approve business-level access requests.  
  

  * Approved WhatsApp marketing templates if you plan to send template-based marketing campaigns.


  


Approved WhatsApp templates are required before users can send template-based outbound WhatsApp messages. Templates are created from **Settings → WhatsApp → Templates** and submitted to Meta for approval.

**How to Upgrade to MM Lite**

  


Upgrading to MM Lite in LeadConnector is quick and doesn’t disrupt your existing campaigns. Follow these steps:

  * Log in to your sub-account  
  

  * Navigate to Settings → WhatsApp

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  


**Q: What is Marketing Messages Lite (MM Lite)?**  
MM Lite is Meta’s enhanced messaging system designed to improve the delivery of WhatsApp marketing messages. It uses intelligent optimization to help messages reach users who are more likely to engage, resulting in stronger campaign performance.

  


  


**Q: How is MM Lite different from the WhatsApp Cloud API?**  
The traditional WhatsApp Cloud API sends messages without the same marketing delivery optimization. MM Lite helps improve marketing performance by:

  * Prioritizing users with higher engagement potential
  * Optimizing delivery timing
  * Improving overall campaign performance


  


  


**Q: Will upgrading to MM Lite affect my existing campaigns?**  
No. Upgrading through LeadConnector does not disrupt your current WhatsApp setup. Existing templates remain unchanged, automations and workflows continue running, and campaigns are not impacted.

  


  


**Q: Do I need technical knowledge to enable MM Lite?**  
No. The upgrade process does not require coding, API changes, or technical expertise. It can be completed through the guided setup flow.

  


  


**Q: Can I still use WhatsApp for customer support after upgrading?**  
Yes. MM Lite is designed specifically for marketing messages. You can continue using standard WhatsApp messaging for:

  * Customer conversations
  * Support queries
  * Transactional messages
  * Service-related communication


  


  


**Q: Will MM Lite improve my message delivery rates?**  
MM Lite is designed to improve delivery performance for eligible WhatsApp marketing messages. Results may vary depending on your audience, message quality, engagement history, and use case.

  


  


**Q: What is Time-to-Live (TTL), and why is it important?**  
TTL defines how long a message remains eligible for delivery. If a message becomes irrelevant, such as an expired offer, it will not be delivered after the defined delivery window. This helps maintain a better user experience.

  


  


**Q: How does MM Lite help prevent spamming users?**  
MM Lite uses engagement signals and frequency controls to optimize message delivery. This helps reduce over-messaging, prevent user fatigue, and support more relevant communication.

  


  


**Q: Do I need access to Facebook Business Manager for the upgrade?**  
Yes. Access to your Facebook Business Manager is required to approve permissions and complete the MM Lite upgrade.

  


  


**Q: Can I track conversions and campaign performance with MM Lite?**  
Yes. MM Lite can work with tools such as Meta Pixel and Conversions API to help track conversions, measure ROI, and optimize future campaigns.

  


  


**Q: Is MM Lite suitable for all types of messages?**  
No. MM Lite is designed for marketing use cases, such as promotions, offers, and campaign broadcasts. For transactional, utility, authentication, or service-related messages, continue using standard WhatsApp messaging.

  


  


**Q: How long does it take to see results after enabling MM Lite?**  
You may start seeing results within a few campaigns. As the system gathers engagement data, performance and delivery may continue to improve over time.

  


  


**Q: Does MM Lite support all types of marketing messaging?**  
Yes. MM Lite supports multiple marketing message use cases, including:

  * 1:1 marketing conversations, such as personalized outreach and offers
  * Bulk campaigns, such as broadcast promotions and announcements
  * Workflow automation, such as drip campaigns, follow-ups, and lead nurturing


  


This allows you to manage eligible WhatsApp marketing communication within HighLevel.
