# WhatsApp Schedule Send

Schedule WhatsApp templates and free-form messages for timely, efficient customer communication

Table of Contents

  * • How to Schedule Send WhatsApp Templates
  * • How to Schedule Send Free-Form WhatsApp Messages
  * • Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


The WhatsApp Schedule Send feature enables you to easily schedule messages for your customers. With this functionality, businesses can schedule both WhatsApp templates and free-form messages, ensuring timely and efficient communication.

## How to Schedule Send WhatsApp Templates

1

Go to **Conversations** , select the **contact** , select **WhatsApp** , then select the **template**.

2

Click the button next to **Send**.

3

Select the date and time to send the WhatsApp template, then click **Send Schedule**.

    Note: Date and time should be in the future.

## How to Schedule Send Free-Form WhatsApp Messages
    
    
    Note: In order to send a free-form WhatsApp message, an open Customer Service Window needs to be there with the customer.

1

Go to **Conversations** , select the **contact** , select **WhatsApp** , then type the free-form message.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155032619943/original/-geYGRhmreNjBWPrlWV8OZfU6Njp6mCJ3Q.png?1725985371)

Click the button next to **Send** , then select the date and time.
    
    
    Note: You can schedule messages up to 24 hours from the last inbound message received from the customer.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Can I schedule a WhatsApp template message for later?

Yes. You can schedule a WhatsApp template message by selecting the template inside the Conversations tab, clicking the calendar icon next to Send, and choosing the desired future date and time.

Q2. Can I schedule free-form WhatsApp messages too?

Yes, but only within an active Customer Service Window. Free-form messages can be scheduled up to 24 hours from the customer's last inbound message.

Q3. What is a Customer Service Window?

It's a 24-hour period that begins when a customer sends you a message. During this window, you can send free-form replies without using pre-approved templates.

Q4. What happens if I try to schedule a free-form message outside the 24-hour window?

The system will not allow scheduling outside the open window. In this case, you'll need to use a WhatsApp template instead.

Q5. How do I know if my message has been successfully scheduled?

After selecting the send date and time, click "Schedule Send." A confirmation will appear in the chat showing the scheduled message with its timestamp.

Q6. Can I edit or cancel a scheduled message?

Currently, scheduled messages cannot be edited. To make changes, delete the scheduled message and create a new one.
