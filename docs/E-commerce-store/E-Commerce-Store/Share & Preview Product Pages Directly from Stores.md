# Share & Preview Product Pages Directly from Stores

Quickly preview and share live product detail page links without opening the Store Builder.

What You'll Learn

You can now preview or copy the live Product Detail Page (PDP) link directly from the Stores → Products section, eliminating the need to open the Store Builder every time you want to share a product.

This article explains how to use the Preview and Copy link actions, how HighLevel resolves product URLs using custom domains and SEO handles, and what to expect when products are mapped to custom PDPs.

## What is Product Page Sharing?

Product Page Sharing allows you to quickly preview and copy live Product Detail Page (PDP) links directly from the Stores → Products section. This streamlines your workflow by eliminating the need to open the Store Builder every time you want to share or verify a product page.

The feature provides two core actions for each included product: Preview opens the live product page in a new browser tab, while Copy link copies the resolved URL to your clipboard and displays a confirmation message.

HighLevel automatically respects custom PDP mappings and resolves URLs using your store's custom domain or preview domain, ensuring that the links you share are accurate and ready for distribution.

2

## Key Benefits

Product Page Sharing delivers several workflow and accuracy improvements for store owners:

**Faster Workflow** — Preview and share product links without leaving the Products section or opening the Store Builder.

**Accurate URLs** — HighLevel automatically uses custom PDP mappings and SEO handles to generate correct product page links.

**Domain Resolution** — URLs prioritize your custom domain, falling back to the preview domain when no custom domain is connected.

**Copy Confirmation** — When you use Copy link, HighLevel displays a "Product link copied" message so you know the URL is ready to paste.

**Improved Builder Previews** — Products mapped to a custom PDP now open the correct PDP in the builder preview instead of the Product List Page.

3

## How Product URLs are Resolved

HighLevel uses a consistent URL resolution process to ensure that product links are accurate and reflect your store's domain and page configuration:

Factor 1

Custom PDP Mapping

If a product is mapped to a custom Product Detail Page, HighLevel automatically uses that page instead of the default PDP template when generating the product URL.

Factor 2

SEO Handle

The product's SEO handle is used as the URL slug, creating clean, search-engine-friendly URLs that match your product naming conventions.

Factor 3

Domain Priority

URLs resolve using your custom domain first. If no custom domain is connected to the store, HighLevel automatically uses the preview domain instead.

Pro Tip

To ensure product links use your branded domain, connect a custom domain to your store before sharing product pages with customers or marketing campaigns.

## How to Preview & Copy Product Links

Follow these steps to preview or share a live product page link from the Products section:

Step 1

Navigate to the Products Section

In your HighLevel account, go to **Stores** → **Products**. The section opens on the **Included** tab by default, displaying all products currently included in your store.

Step 2

Locate the Product

Find the product you want to preview or share from the list of included products. Only products in the **Included** tab display the Preview and Copy link actions.

Step 3

Choose an Action

Each product offers two actions:

  * **Preview** — Opens the live Product Detail Page in a new browser tab so you can verify the page before sharing.
  * **Copy link** — Copies the resolved product URL to your clipboard and displays a "Product link copied" confirmation message.

Step 4

Share or Verify the Link

After copying the link, paste it into your marketing campaigns, email sequences, or social media posts. If you used Preview, review the live page to ensure it displays correctly before sharing with customers.

Note

Preview and Copy link actions are available only for products in the **Included** tab. Products in the **Excluded** or **All** tabs do not display these actions.

## Updated Product Tabs & Preview Behavior

The Products section includes several interface and behavior improvements to streamline product management and preview workflows:

Enhancement 1

Default Tab Order

The Products section opens on the **Included** tab by default, followed by **Excluded** and **All**. This prioritizes the products that are live and available for preview or sharing.

Enhancement 2

Included Products Only

Preview and Copy link actions are available only for products in the **Included** tab. This ensures you only share or preview products that are currently active in your store.

Enhancement 3

Correct Builder Previews

When you preview a product from the Store Builder, HighLevel now opens the correct Product Detail Page for products mapped to a custom PDP, instead of defaulting to the Product List Page.

Result

These improvements create a more intuitive workflow by keeping live products front and center and ensuring that previews always reflect the correct product page configuration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I preview or copy links for excluded products?

No. Preview and Copy link actions are available only for products in the **Included** tab. Excluded products do not display these actions because they are not currently live in your store.

Q: What happens if I don't have a custom domain connected to my store?

If no custom domain is connected, HighLevel automatically uses the available preview domain to generate the product URL. The link will still work, but it will use HighLevel's default preview domain instead of your branded domain.

Q: How does HighLevel determine which Product Detail Page to use?

If a product is mapped to a custom PDP, HighLevel automatically uses that custom page when generating the product URL. If no custom PDP is mapped, HighLevel uses the default PDP template for your store.

Q: What does the "Product link copied" confirmation mean?

This confirmation appears when you use the Copy link action and indicates that the product URL has been successfully copied to your clipboard. You can now paste the link into your marketing materials or share it with customers.

Q: Can I preview products from the Store Builder?

Yes. When you preview a product from the Store Builder, HighLevel opens the correct Product Detail Page based on the product's custom PDP mapping. This ensures you see the actual page your customers will view.

Q: What is the SEO handle, and how does it affect product URLs?

The SEO handle is a URL-friendly version of your product name. HighLevel uses the SEO handle as the URL slug for each product, creating clean, readable URLs that improve search engine optimization and make it easier for customers to share product links.

Q: Do I need to open the Store Builder to share a product link?

No. You can preview and copy product links directly from the **Stores** → **Products** section without opening the Store Builder. This streamlines your workflow and saves time when sharing multiple products.

Q: Can I preview products that are not yet live in my store?

No. Preview and Copy link actions are available only for products in the **Included** tab. To preview a product, you must first include it in your store from the Products section.
