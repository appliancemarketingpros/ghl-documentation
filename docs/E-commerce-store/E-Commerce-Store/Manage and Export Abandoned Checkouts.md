## What Are Abandoned Checkouts?

Abandoned Checkouts occur when customers add products to their cart and begin the checkout process but leave before completing their purchase. HighLevel saves these incomplete checkouts so you can review customer and cart details, monitor recovery progress, and encourage customers to return and complete their order.

The Abandoned Checkouts page provides a centralized view of these records, allowing you to search, filter, export, and manage abandoned checkouts from one place. You can also review checkout details, configure abandoned cart notifications, and copy a recovery link to manually share with customers when needed.

2

## Key Features of the Abandoned Checkouts Page

The Abandoned Checkouts page includes powerful tools to help you manage and recover incomplete purchases:

**Export CSV** — Select individual, multiple, or all records on the current page and export customer information, cart contents, product details, activity logs, and recovery status.

**Search & Filters** — Search by customer name or email and filter records by Email Status, Recovery Status, or Date Range.

**Configure Notifications** — Open Abandoned Cart Notification Settings directly from the page to manage automated recovery emails.

**Shareable Recovery Link** — Copy a checkout URL and send it directly to customers so they can quickly resume their purchase.

**Improved Experience** — Updated loading states, pagination, responsive layouts, and empty states provide a faster and more intuitive experience.

## How to Search and Filter Records

Search and filter tools help you quickly locate abandoned checkouts based on customer information or recovery activity. The table updates automatically to display only records that match your search and filter criteria.

**Search for a Customer** — Enter a customer's name or email address in the search field.

**Apply Filters** — Click Add Filter and choose one of the available filters (Email Status, Recovery Status, Date Range).

## How to Export Abandoned Checkout Records

Export abandoned checkout data as a CSV file for reporting, analysis, or record keeping. The export includes customer information, cart contents, product details, activity, and recovery information.

Step 1

Select Records

Use the checkboxes beside individual records to select one or more abandoned checkouts.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077629503/original/Vol0j1kWKpjnTvXR3r_aI1qzkevWcl02eA.png?1785868182)

Step 2

Export the Records

After selecting at least one record, click **Export CSV**.

  * If the export contains 5,000 records or fewer, the CSV downloads immediately.
  * If the export contains more than 5,000 records, HighLevel processes the export and delivers the CSV file by email.


  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077629507/original/ldwkEjGko-W78ginf8aRJha8SqySYrk1cw.png?1785868191)

## How to Share Recovery Links

Recovery links allow customers to return directly to their abandoned checkout without starting over.

Step 1

Open an Abandoned Checkout

Select the abandoned checkout you want to review by clicking on the order's name.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077629578/original/s7Q75aEeFsj3e-mEhp__5BCr8SDXBykZiQ.png?1785868274)

Step 2

Copy the Recovery Link

Locate the checkout URL near the top of the details page and click the **Copy** icon.

You can then paste the recovery link into an email, SMS, or other message so the customer can quickly return to checkout and complete their purchase.

  


![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155077629586/original/Xd_XgWg-Cax3kZMMq3L3zBWz08QUwglQIQ.png?1785868292)

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What happens if I export more than 5,000 records?

HighLevel processes the export and delivers the completed CSV file by email.

Q: What information is included in the CSV export?

The export includes customer information, address details, cart totals, discounts, coupon codes, email and recovery statuses, checkout URLs, store information, abandonment date and time, and individual product details such as product name, quantity, price, and total.

Q: Can I filter abandoned checkouts by recovery status?

Yes. Use Add Filter and choose Recovery Status to narrow the displayed records.

Q: How do I know if a recovery email was sent?

Review the Email Status for each abandoned checkout record.

Q: Can I search abandoned checkouts by date?

Yes. Use the Date Range filter to display abandoned checkouts from a specific period.

Q: Why is the Export CSV button disabled?

The Export CSV button becomes available only after at least one abandoned checkout record has been selected.

Q: Can I manually send a recovery link to a customer?

Yes. Open an abandoned checkout, copy the recovery link, and send it through email, SMS, or another messaging platform.
